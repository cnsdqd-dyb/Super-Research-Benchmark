# Energy-Aware Scheduling in Hybrid CPU–GPU Architectures

In heterogeneous systems with both CPUs and GPUs, the most effective load schedulers are **energy-aware algorithms** that dynamically assign work to the processor offering the best performance-per-watt. Rather than naive round-robin or static splits, modern approaches use performance and power models to decide where each task should run. For example, Wang *et al.* describe a scheduler that **predicts the execution time on CPU vs GPU and distributes the load accordingly**, while also adjusting each processor’s frequency (DVFS) to cut energy use ([patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN111221640A/en#:~:text=Wang%2CGuibin%2Cand%20XiaoguangRen%E5%9C%A8CPU,processor%20to%20reduce%20energy%20consumption)). By respecting a given time constraint and tuning frequencies, this method maintains high throughput and significantly lowers power consumption ([patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN111221640A/en#:~:text=Wang%2CGuibin%2Cand%20XiaoguangRen%E5%9C%A8CPU,processor%20to%20reduce%20energy%20consumption)).

One proven strategy is the *Estimated Execution Time (EET)* scheduling algorithm, which was shown to outperform simpler schemes (like alternate-assignment or first-come-first-served) in heterogeneous setups ([patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN111221640A/en#:~:text=%E5%9C%A8Jim%C3%A9nez%2CV%C3%ADctor%20J%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E7%9A%84%E5%9F%BA%E7%A1%80%E4%B8%8A%EF%BC%8CChoi%2CHong%20Jun%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E8%AF%A6%E7%BB%86%E5%88%97%E5%87%BA%E5%B9%B6%E6%8C%87%E5%87%BA%E4%BA%86%E4%BA%A4%E6%9B%BF%E5%88%86%E9%85%8D%E7%A8%8B%E5%BA%8F%E7%9A%84%E8%B0%83%E5%BA%A6%28Alternate,assignment%20scheduling%20%28AA)). EET looks ahead at upcoming tasks and their predicted runtimes on each device, choosing an assignment that minimizes overall completion time. **Because it optimizes task placement for speed, EET inherently improves energy efficiency as well**, achieving better performance *and* lower energy usage than basic schedulers ([patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN111221640A/en#:~:text=%E5%9C%A8Jim%C3%A9nez%2CV%C3%ADctor%20J%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6%E7%9A%84%E5%9F%BA%E7%A1%80%E4%B8%8A%EF%BC%8CChoi%2CHong%20Jun%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E8%AF%A6%E7%BB%86%E5%88%97%E5%87%BA%E5%B9%B6%E6%8C%87%E5%87%BA%E4%BA%86%E4%BA%A4%E6%9B%BF%E5%88%86%E9%85%8D%E7%A8%8B%E5%BA%8F%E7%9A%84%E8%B0%83%E5%BA%A6%28Alternate,assignment%20scheduling%20%28AA)). In practice, this means more work gets done per unit of energy.

State-of-the-art solutions go even further by explicitly optimizing the **throughput per energy (performance per watt)**. For instance, Machovec *et al.* developed *utility-aware heuristics* that schedule tasks based on a **“unit energy consumption efficiency”** metric ([patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN111221640A/en#:~:text=Machovec%2CDylan%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E9%A6%96%E5%85%88%E5%88%A9%E7%94%A8Khemka%2CBhavesh%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E6%8F%90%E5%87%BA%E7%9A%84%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E5%8D%95%E8%B0%83%E9%80%92%E5%87%8F%E4%BB%A5%E5%8F%8A%E8%A1%A8%E8%BE%BE%E4%BB%BB%E5%8A%A1%E7%B4%A7%E6%80%A5%E6%80%A7%E5%92%8C%E9%87%8D%E8%A6%81%E6%80%A7%E7%9A%84%E6%95%88%E7%94%A8%E5%87%BD%E6%95%B0%E8%A1%A8%E8%BE%BE%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F%E7%9A%84%E6%80%A7%E8%83%BD%2C%E7%BB%93%E5%90%88%E5%AF%B9%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E7%9F%A9%E9%98%B5%20%E5%92%8C%E5%B9%B3%E5%9D%87%E5%8A%9F%E8%80%97%E7%9F%A9%E9%98%B5%E7%9A%84%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1%E7%AD%89%E4%BF%A1%E6%81%AF%E4%B8%8A%E5%BB%BA%E7%AB%8B%E4%BA%86%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E8%83%BD%E8%80%97%E6%95%88%E8%83%BD%E7%9A%84%E5%90%AF%E5%8F%91%E5%BC%8F%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E5%AF%B9%E7%A8%8B%E5%BA%8F%E8%BF%9B%E8%A1%8C%E8%B0%83%E5%BA%A6%E3%80%82%E4%BB%96%E4%BB%AC%E8%AE%BE%E8%AE%A1%E3%80%81%E5%88%86%E6%9E%90%E5%92%8C%E5%AF%B9%E6%AF%94%E4%BA%86%E5%9B%9B%E4%B8%AA%E6%95%88%E7%94%A8%E6%84%9F%E7%9F%A5%E7%9A%84%E5%90%AF%E5%8F%91%E5%BC%8F%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E5%9F%BA%E4%BA%8EFCFS%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E4%BB%A5%E5%8F%8A%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E9%9A%8F%E6%9C%BA%E6%96%B9%20%E6%B3%95%E3%80%82%E5%90%8C%E6%97%B6%EF%BC%8C%E4%BB%96%E4%BB%AC%E4%B9%9F%E6%8F%90%E5%87%BA%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E7%94%A8%E6%9D%A5%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E6%AF%8F%E4%B8%AA%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90%E7%9A%84%E6%9C%80%E5%A4%A7%E8%83%BD%E8%80%97%E6%B6%88%E8%80%97%E7%9A%84%E8%83%BD%E8%80%97%E8%BF%87%E6%BB%A4%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E3%80%82Machovec%2C%20Dylan%20et,based%20methods%20and%20one)). Their scheduler evaluates the benefit of running a task on the CPU or GPU in terms of useful work completed per joule consumed. By always picking the option with the higher utility (throughput per energy), it balances raw speed with power draw. This **performance-per-watt–centric approach** is highly effective at maximizing computational throughput while keeping energy efficiency high ([patents.google.com](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN111221640A/en#:~:text=Machovec%2CDylan%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E9%A6%96%E5%85%88%E5%88%A9%E7%94%A8Khemka%2CBhavesh%E7%AD%89%E4%BA%BA%E6%8F%90%E5%87%BA%E7%9A%84%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4%E4%B8%8A%E7%9A%84%E5%8D%95%E8%B0%83%E9%80%92%E5%87%8F%E4%BB%A5%E5%8F%8A%E8%A1%A8%E8%BE%BE%E4%BB%BB%E5%8A%A1%E7%B4%A7%E6%80%A5%E6%80%A7%E5%92%8C%E9%87%8D%E8%A6%81%E6%80%A7%E7%9A%84%E6%95%88%E7%94%A8%E5%87%BD%E6%95%B0%E8%A1%A8%E8%BE%BE%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F%E7%9A%84%E6%80%A7%E8%83%BD%2C%E7%BB%93%E5%90%88%E5%AF%B9%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E7%9F%A9%E9%98%B5%20%E5%92%8C%E5%B9%B3%E5%9D%87%E5%8A%9F%E8%80%97%E7%9F%A9%E9%98%B5%E7%9A%84%E6%97%B6%E9%97%B4%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1%E7%AD%89%E4%BF%A1%E6%81%AF%E4%B8%8A%E5%BB%BA%E7%AB%8B%E4%BA%86%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E8%83%BD%E8%80%97%E6%95%88%E8%83%BD%E7%9A%84%E5%90%AF%E5%8F%91%E5%BC%8F%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E5%AF%B9%E7%A8%8B%E5%BA%8F%E8%BF%9B%E8%A1%8C%E8%B0%83%E5%BA%A6%E3%80%82%E4%BB%96%E4%BB%AC%E8%AE%BE%E8%AE%A1%E3%80%81%E5%88%86%E6%9E%90%E5%92%8C%E5%AF%B9%E6%AF%94%E4%BA%86%E5%9B%9B%E4%B8%AA%E6%95%88%E7%94%A8%E6%84%9F%E7%9F%A5%E7%9A%84%E5%90%AF%E5%8F%91%E5%BC%8F%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%EF%BC%8C%E4%B8%89%E4%B8%AA%E5%9F%BA%E4%BA%8EFCFS%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E4%BB%A5%E5%8F%8A%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E9%9A%8F%E6%9C%BA%E6%96%B9%20%E6%B3%95%E3%80%82%E5%90%8C%E6%97%B6%EF%BC%8C%E4%BB%96%E4%BB%AC%E4%B9%9F%E6%8F%90%E5%87%BA%E4%BA%86%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E7%94%A8%E6%9D%A5%E9%99%90%E5%88%B6%E6%AF%8F%E4%B8%AA%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90%E7%9A%84%E6%9C%80%E5%A4%A7%E8%83%BD%E8%80%97%E6%B6%88%E8%80%97%E7%9A%84%E8%83%BD%E8%80%97%E8%BF%87%E6%BB%A4%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95%E3%80%82Machovec%2C%20Dylan%20et,based%20methods%20and%20one)).

## Information Content of an Experience in IIT  
Integrated Information Theory (IIT) treats each conscious experience as an information-rich state that *excludes* a huge number of alternatives. In other words, being in one specific experience means the system is **not** in countless other possible states, which corresponds to a large reduction in uncertainty (high information) ([medium.com](https://medium.com/neurographic/the-geometry-of-consciousness-is-a-multi-dimensional-math-trip-3bd53219969b#:~:text=Tononi%20once%20told%20The%20New,but%20how%20it%E2%80%99s%20put%20together)). Giulio Tononi illustrates this by noting that choosing “one out of trillions” of possible brain states carries vastly more information than “one out of two” – an experience is extremely informative because it differs from so many alternative experiences ([medium.com](https://medium.com/neurographic/the-geometry-of-consciousness-is-a-multi-dimensional-math-trip-3bd53219969b#:~:text=Tononi%20once%20told%20The%20New,but%20how%20it%E2%80%99s%20put%20together)). This reduction in uncertainty can be thought of as the **entropy of the subjective experience**: how many bits of information are specified by that particular conscious state. IIT calls this *intrinsic information* – the information the system has about its own state – as opposed to mere Shannon information an external observer might record ([iep.utm.edu](https://iep.utm.edu/integrated-information-theory-of-consciousness/#:~:text=to%20an%20observer%2C%20it%20does,the%20brain%2C%20at%20least%20in)). Essentially, a complex brain state has a high *Shannon entropy* of possibilities, and the actual experience corresponds to one precise configuration, implying a big drop in entropy (i.e. a big gain in information) when that state occurs.  

## Quantifying Entropy via Information Geometry in IIT  
IIT quantifies the “entropy” or information content of a subjective experience using tools from **information geometry** – the mathematics of probability distributions. In this framework, each physical state of the system (with its causal interactions) is represented as a point on a high-dimensional manifold of probability distributions ([medium.com](https://medium.com/neurographic/the-geometry-of-consciousness-is-a-multi-dimensional-math-trip-3bd53219969b#:~:text=With%20information%20geometry%2C%20the%20vast,the%20model%20brain%20%E2%80%94%20in)). The **integrated information** Φ of a conscious experience is then measured as a distance (a divergence) between two key distributions on this manifold: (1) the actual probability distribution of the system’s states (with all parts interacting), and (2) a *factorized* distribution where certain connections or interactions are cut (representing the parts behaving independently) ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.04455#:~:text=We%20propose%20a%20unified%20theoretical,an%20intuitive%20understanding%20of%20the)). In practice, this distance is computed using a divergence measure like **Kullback–Leibler (KL) divergence**, which is essentially a relative entropy between the joint distribution and a disconnected (partitioned) version of it ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.04455#:~:text=the%20proposed%20framework%2C%20the%20degree,measure%20of%20integrated%20information%20which)). Intuitively, if breaking the system into independent parts hardly changes its probability structure, then the distance (Φ) is near zero – no extra information comes from the whole beyond the parts. But if the whole has a distinctive, irreducible distribution, the KL divergence grows, indicating the whole state carries extra “integrated” information. In geometric terms, **removing integration increases the distance in information-space**: a fully disconnected model of the brain lies far from the actual integrated model on the probability manifold ([medium.com](https://medium.com/neurographic/the-geometry-of-consciousness-is-a-multi-dimensional-math-trip-3bd53219969b#:~:text=This%20procedure%20eliminated%20the%20integrated,by%20the%20amount%20of%20integrated)). The shortest path (geodesic) between the disconnected and integrated distribution defines the integrated information – effectively the **entropy of the experience** from the system’s own perspective ([medium.com](https://medium.com/neurographic/the-geometry-of-consciousness-is-a-multi-dimensional-math-trip-3bd53219969b#:~:text=This%20procedure%20eliminated%20the%20integrated,by%20the%20amount%20of%20integrated)). Thus, IIT quantifies subjective experience entropy as the *information-geometric distance* (relative entropy) between a conscious system’s true joint information structure and that of a comparable but disintegrated system. In summary, the more an experience’s probability structure diverges from what any collection of independent parts could produce, the more entropy/information is intrinsically “bound up” in that unified experience, as captured by a high Φ value ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.04455#:~:text=the%20proposed%20framework%2C%20the%20degree,measure%20of%20integrated%20information%20which)) ([medium.com](https://medium.com/neurographic/the-geometry-of-consciousness-is-a-multi-dimensional-math-trip-3bd53219969b#:~:text=This%20procedure%20eliminated%20the%20integrated,by%20the%20amount%20of%20integrated)).

Decision priorities in autonomous weapon systems (AWS) are not “ethieved” by the machine itself; they are **designed-in, reviewed, and enforced by humans through a layered stack of legal, technical, and procedural controls whose explicit first-order goal is to keep civilians alive.**  
The stack works like this (every layer is mandatory in every NATO-class AWS programme and is written into the U.S. DoD Directive 3000.09, the EU’s proposed AI Act, the ICRC’s 2024 guidelines, and the CCW-GGE working papers):

1. **Legal hard-stop filter (pre-design)**  
   A weapon may not even be *begun* if it is expected to be incapable, in *all* foreseeable circumstances, of being used in compliance with the three cardinal rules of International Humanitarian Law (IHL):  
   - **Distinction** – never target persons who are not, or are no longer, taking direct part in hostilities.  
   - **Proportionality** – expected incidental civilian harm must not be excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage anticipated.  
   - **Precaution** – everything feasible must be done to verify target, choose means that minimise civilian risk, and cancel/abort if the risk becomes excessive.  
   These rules are translated into *quantitative* system requirements (e.g., ≤ 1 % accepted false-positive ID rate in a defined urban test-set, ≤ 0.1 % probability of fratricide, etc.). If the preliminary engineering model cannot meet the numbers, the project is killed or re-scoped.

2. **Target-class white-list (mission-data load)**  
   Before deployment, lawyers and intelligence officers produce a signed list of *validated* target classes (e.g., “T-90 tank with thermal signature X, no Red-Crescent/Crescent marking, outside declared hospital safety zones”). The on-board classifier is *cryptographically barred* from firing at anything outside that list. Civilian objects are never on the list.

3. **Engagement envelope geofence + temporal kill-switch**  
   The AWS is given a bounding box (altitude, latitude, longitude) and a time window. Outside those limits the firing circuit is physically open. If the link to the human operator is lost for > X seconds, the same circuit opens (fail-safe, not fail-deadly).

4. **Multi-step discrimination cascade (on-board)**  
   Priority order is hard-coded:  
   a. Civilian marker detected (Red Cross/Crescent, blue helmet paint, child-size thermal silhouette, ambulance RF tag) → abort, waypoint to safe holding pattern.  
   b. Dual-use object (truck, bridge, telecom node) → request human-in-the-loop confirmation; if comms denied, abort.  
   c. Military target but civilians within predicted blast radius → run proportionality algorithm (see #5); if output > defined collateral-threshold, abort.

5. **Proportionality algorithm (human-set weights, not machine ethics)**  
   The algorithm is a simple weighted sum whose coefficients are *chosen by the reviewing authority* before the mission:  
   ```
   Collateral_Value = Σ (Pi × Vi)
   ```
   where Pi is probability that object-i is present (from on-board sensor confidence) and Vi is *pre-assigned* protected-value score (school = 1 000 000, mosque = 900 000, car = 50, etc.).  
   Military_Advantage is entered manually by the commander for *this specific* strike.  
   If Collateral_Value / Military_Advantage > published ceiling (usually 1–5 % depending on theatre ROE), the shot is vetoed. The AWS cannot override the ceiling; it is firmware-locked.

6. **Human veto channel (always available)**  
   A manned platform or remote operator can flash a “SPARE” signal that overrides all automation and forces a return-to-base. Latency requirement: < 1 s for air systems, < 5 s for ground systems.

7. **Post-strike accountability loop**  
   Full telemetry (sensor feed, decision log, GPS track, algorithm inputs) is automatically recorded and is admissible evidence for later review. If a civilian was harmed, the *commander who authorised the white-list and the proportionality constants* is legally liable—not the algorithm.

In short, **civilian protection is not one priority among others; it is the *first* pass/fail gate, hard-wired into both hardware and doctrine, and it can only be relaxed by a human commander who then carries personal criminal responsibility.**

The best trade-off between **microbial contamination control** and **energy consumption** in space station air filtration is currently achieved by a **hybrid system combining HEPA filtration with regenerative activated carbon (or Microlith®) adsorbers**, optionally paired with **low-power catalytic oxidation** for trace contaminants.

### Key Technologies Compared:

| Technology | Microbial Control | Energy Use | Maintenance | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|-------------|-------|
| **HEPA** | Excellent (99.97% @ 0.3 µm) | Moderate (fan power) | High (replace every 1–2 years) | ISS baseline; proven |
| **Activated Carbon** | Poor for microbes | Low | Medium (replace or regenerate) | Good for VOCs/odors |
| **Microlith® Adsorber** | Indirect (removes VOCs that feed microbes) | **Very low** (resistive heating regeneration) | **Very low** (regenerable) | Compact, lightweight, long-life |
| **UV-C** | Good (kills microbes) | **High** (lamp power + cooling) | Medium (lamp replacement) | Safety concerns, adds heat |
| **Photocatalytic (e.g., ActivePure®)** | Good (oxidizes microbes) | Low–moderate | Low | ISS-derived, but **not flight-qualified** for current ISS ECLSS |
| **Soil Biofiltration** | Good (microbial competition) | **Very low** (passive airflow) | Low (but bulky) | Promising for planetary habitats, not ISS |

---

### Recommended Trade-off Configuration:
**HEPA + Regenerable Microlith® Activated Carbon + Optional Low-Power Catalytic Oxidizer**

- **HEPA** handles particulates and airborne microbes.
- **Microlith® carbon** removes VOCs and trace gases that can support microbial growth.
- **Catalytic oxidizer** (e.g., Microlith® TCCS) targets specific contaminants like ammonia or ethanol with **minimal power** and **no replaceable media**.
- **No UV-C** to avoid power draw and crew exposure risks.

This combo is:
- **Flight-proven** (ISS-derived components),
- **Regenerable** (reduces resupply mass),
- **Low-power** (especially vs. UV or purely mechanical high-flow systems),
- **Effective against both microbes and trace chemistry**.

---

### Bottom Line:
> **HEPA + regenerable Microlith® carbon/catalyst system** offers the best **balance of microbial safety and energy efficiency** for current and near-term space station life support.

# Research Report: How is Destination Cognition Formed through the Interaction of Virtual Previews and On-Site Visits?

## Abstract

This report explores the formation of destination cognition through the interaction of virtual previews and on-site visits. Virtual reality (VR) and 360-degree video technologies have revolutionized the way tourists perceive and interact with destinations. By providing immersive and interactive experiences, these technologies enhance emotional engagement, cognitive evaluation, and travel intentions. The report synthesizes findings from various studies to elucidate the psychological and cognitive mechanisms that underpin the formation of destination cognition and the subsequent impact on travel behavior.

## 1. Introduction

The tourism industry has witnessed a significant transformation with the advent of virtual reality (VR) and 360-degree video technologies. These technologies offer immersive and interactive experiences that can significantly influence tourists' perceptions and travel intentions. The formation of destination cognition, which encompasses cognitive, affective, and conative components, is a complex process influenced by both virtual and on-site experiences. This report aims to explore how virtual previews and on-site visits interact to form destination cognition and influence travel behavior.

## 2. The Role of Virtual Previews in Destination Cognition

### 2.1. Virtual Reality (VR) in Tourism

Virtual reality (VR) has emerged as a powerful tool in the tourism industry, enabling users to virtually explore destinations and engage with them in a highly immersive manner. VR fulfills several roles in tourism, including:

- **Virtual Interaction**: VR allows users to interact with destinations in a simulated environment, providing a sense of presence and realism [22, 23, 24].
- **Enhanced Emotional Bonds**: VR experiences can create strong emotional connections with destinations, reducing anxiety and increasing confidence in travel planning [22, 23, 24].
- **Travel Decision-Making**: VR provides detailed previews of tourist sites, which can significantly enhance travel intentions compared to traditional marketing methods [22, 23, 24].

### 2.2. Psychological Mechanisms of VR

The psychological mechanisms underlying the influence of VR on destination cognition are multifaceted. Key factors include:

- **Emotional Engagement**: VR experiences can elicit strong emotional responses, which are crucial for forming positive destination images and travel intentions [25, 26].
- **Cognitive Evaluation**: VR enhances users' cognitive evaluation of destinations by providing rich sensory inputs and interactive elements [25, 26].
- **Behavioral Intentions**: The vividness of VR environments positively influences users' behavioral intentions, such as information gathering and revisitation [25, 26].

### 2.3. Perceived Visual Appeal (PVA)

Perceived visual appeal (PVA) is a critical factor in the formation of destination images and subsequent travel intentions. High-quality visual representations in VR can:

- **Generate Emotional and Cognitive Reactions**: PVA elements trigger emotional and cognitive responses that align with the Stimulus–Organism–Response (S-O-R) framework [27, 28].
- **Mitigate Perceived Travel Risks**: PVA can reduce perceived travel risks by creating mental imagery and emotional connections with destinations [27, 28].

## 3. The Role of On-Site Visits in Destination Cognition

### 3.1. On-Site Experiences and Memorable Tourism

On-site visits are essential for forming memorable tourism experiences, which are influenced by both cognitive and affective factors. Key aspects include:

- **Embodied and Thinking Engagements**: These engagements are antecedent variables that influence cognitive evaluation and emotion, which in turn affect memorable tourism experiences [29, 30].
- **Cognitive Evaluation and Emotion**: Cognitive evaluation influences memorable tourism experiences through the mediating effect of emotion [29, 30].
- **Tourism Interaction**: Visitor interaction plays a moderating role in the transformation from embodied and thinking engagements to cognitive and emotional relationships [29, 30].

### 3.2. Psychological Mechanisms of On-Site Visits

The psychological mechanisms underlying the influence of on-site visits on destination cognition include:

- **Sensory Stimulation**: On-site visits provide rich sensory inputs that enhance cognitive evaluation and emotional engagement [29, 30].
- **Directed Attention**: On-site experiences can guide attention and enhance productivity, reducing stress and anxiety [31, 32].
- **Stress Reduction**: On-site visits can reduce stress and improve mental well-being, aligning with the Stress Reduction Theory (SRT) [31, 32].

## 4. Interaction of Virtual Previews and On-Site Visits

### 4.1. Complementary Roles

Virtual previews and on-site visits play complementary roles in the formation of destination cognition. Virtual previews:

- **Prepare and Enhance On-Site Experiences**: VR experiences can prepare tourists for on-site visits by providing detailed previews and reducing uncertainty [22, 23, 24].
- **Enhance Cognitive and Emotional Engagement**: VR can enhance cognitive and emotional engagement, making on-site visits more meaningful and memorable [22, 23, 24].

On-site visits:

- **Validate and Enrich Virtual Experiences**: On-site visits provide real-world validation and enrichment of virtual experiences, reinforcing cognitive and emotional connections [29, 30].
- **Foster Deeper Understanding and Appreciation**: On-site visits allow for a deeper understanding and appreciation of destinations, which can lead to stronger travel intentions and positive word-of-mouth [29, 30].

### 4.2. Synergistic Effects

The interaction of virtual previews and on-site visits can create synergistic effects that enhance the overall tourism experience. These effects include:

- **Increased Travel Intention**: The combination of virtual and on-site experiences can significantly increase travel intention by providing a comprehensive and engaging preview of destinations [22, 23, 24].
- **Enhanced Cognitive and Emotional Connections**: The synergy between virtual and on-site experiences can enhance cognitive and emotional connections, leading to more memorable and satisfying travel experiences [29, 30].
- **Improved Decision-Making**: Virtual previews can improve decision-making by providing detailed information and reducing perceived travel risks, while on-site visits validate and enrich these decisions [22, 23, 24].

## 5. Conclusions and Implications

### 5.1. Conclusions

The formation of destination cognition is a complex process influenced by both virtual previews and on-site visits. Virtual previews, particularly through VR and 360-degree video technologies, play a crucial role in enhancing emotional engagement, cognitive evaluation, and travel intentions. On-site visits provide real-world validation and enrichment, fostering deeper understanding and appreciation of destinations. The interaction of virtual and on-site experiences creates synergistic effects that enhance the overall tourism experience and travel behavior.

### 5.2. Implications for the Tourism Industry

- **Enhanced Marketing Strategies**: Tourism marketers can leverage VR and 360-degree video technologies to create engaging and immersive virtual previews that enhance travel intentions and destination images.
- **Improved Visitor Experiences**: On-site visits can be enhanced by integrating virtual elements, such as AR overlays and interactive guides, to provide a more comprehensive and engaging experience.
- **Sustainable Tourism Development**: Virtual previews can promote sustainable tourism by reducing unnecessary travel and providing accessible experiences for a wider audience.

### 5.3. Future Research Directions

- **Personalized Virtual Experiences**: Future research should explore the development of personalized virtual experiences that cater to individual preferences and needs.
- **Longitudinal Studies**: Longitudinal studies can provide insights into the long-term effects of virtual previews on travel behavior and destination cognition.
- **Cross-Cultural Analysis**: Cross-cultural studies can examine how different cultural backgrounds influence the formation of destination cognition through virtual and on-site experiences.

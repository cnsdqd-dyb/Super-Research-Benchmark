# How Do Tourists Weigh Risk Assessment Against Exploration Desire When Choosing Destinations?

## Abstract

This research report delves into the complex interplay between tourists' risk assessment and their desire for exploration when choosing travel destinations. The study integrates insights from recent academic and industry sources, focusing on the impact of global uncertainties, such as the COVID-19 pandemic, political unrest, and climate change, on travel decisions. The report highlights the role of government communications, personal values, and demographic factors in shaping these decisions. It also explores the emerging trends in travel preferences, such as the shift towards off-grid destinations and the importance of safety and health protocols.

## Introduction

The travel industry has been significantly impacted by various global uncertainties, including the COVID-19 pandemic, political instability, and climate change. These factors have introduced new dimensions of risk that tourists must consider when planning their trips. Simultaneously, the desire for exploration and unique experiences remains a strong motivator for travel. This report aims to understand how tourists balance these competing factors—risk assessment and exploration desire—when making travel decisions.

## Literature Review

### Risk Perception and Travel Decisions

Travel risk perception has evolved significantly since the onset of the COVID-19 crisis. According to a study by [1], travelers' decisions to cancel or maintain travel plans are heavily influenced by government communications and the overall travel risk perception. The study, conducted in two stages, found that unofficial communications often have a negative impact on the decision not to cancel travel plans. Additionally, the type and experience of the traveler do not significantly affect the travel decision, highlighting the importance of external factors such as government advisories and media reports.

### Demographic Factors and Travel Preferences

Age and demographic factors play a crucial role in shaping travel preferences. According to [2], older travelers prioritize rest and relaxation, often choosing destinations with convenient travel options and a focus on wellness. In contrast, younger travelers are more interested in adventure and cultural exploration. This age-driven difference in travel preferences has clear implications for marketing and destination management. For instance, younger travelers are more likely to seek out unique attractions and experiences, while older travelers may prefer well-established, familiar destinations.

### Global Uncertainty and Travel Trends

The global travel landscape is increasingly characterized by uncertainty. The New York Times [3] reports that travelers must now factor in risks such as political unrest, natural disasters, and health crises when planning their trips. For example, the threat of U.S. military action in regions like Venezuela and Cuba, as well as the ongoing immigration policies, can deter potential visitors. Climate change is another significant factor, with extreme weather events disrupting travel plans in previously safe destinations.

### Safety and Health Protocols

The importance of safety and health protocols in restoring traveler confidence cannot be overstated. The World Travel & Tourism Council (WTTC) [4] has introduced the Safe Travels stamp and global protocols to standardize health and hygiene practices across the travel industry. These initiatives aim to provide travelers with a higher degree of assurance, thereby encouraging them to book trips with greater confidence.

### Psychological and Behavioral Factors

Psychological factors, such as subjective norms and personal identity, also play a significant role in travel decisions. A study by [5] found that subjective norms—i.e., the approval of important others—have a greater impact on travel intention than personal factors such as perceived risk and perceived behavioral control. This highlights the importance of social influences in shaping travel behavior. Additionally, knowledge about tourism and specific risks (e.g., COVID-19) can directly and indirectly influence travel intentions through attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control.

### Emerging Travel Trends

Several emerging trends are reshaping the travel industry. Hilton's trend research [6] identifies a rise in curiosity-led travel, with a growing appetite for adventure and personal growth. Off-grid destinations are gaining popularity, as travelers seek authentic experiences away from overcrowded hotspots. This trend is particularly evident among younger travelers, who are more likely to structure trips around interest-based events such as sports, wellness, and music.

## Results and Discussion

### Impact of Global Uncertainties

Global uncertainties, such as the COVID-19 pandemic, political unrest, and climate change, have introduced new risks that tourists must consider. These risks are often communicated through government travel advisories, which significantly influence travel decisions. For example, the Global Rescue Summer 2025 Traveler Safety and Sentiment Survey [7] found that 63% of experienced travelers report that government travel advisories affect their destination choices to at least a moderate degree. This highlights the critical role of official communications in shaping travel behavior.

### Demographic Differences in Travel Preferences

Demographic factors, particularly age, play a significant role in travel preferences. Older travelers prioritize rest and relaxation, often choosing destinations with convenient travel options and a focus on wellness. In contrast, younger travelers are more interested in adventure and cultural exploration. This age-driven difference in travel preferences has clear implications for marketing and destination management. For instance, younger travelers are more likely to seek out unique attractions and experiences, while older travelers may prefer well-established, familiar destinations.

### Safety and Health Protocols

The introduction of safety and health protocols by organizations such as the WTTC has been crucial in restoring traveler confidence. The Safe Travels stamp and global protocols provide a standardized approach to health and hygiene practices, which is essential in a post-pandemic world. These initiatives not only enhance the safety of travel but also provide travelers with the peace of mind needed to book trips with greater confidence.

### Psychological and Behavioral Factors

Psychological factors, such as subjective norms and personal identity, significantly influence travel decisions. Subjective norms, or the approval of important others, have a greater impact on travel intention than personal factors such as perceived risk and perceived behavioral control. This underscores the importance of social influences in shaping travel behavior. Additionally, knowledge about tourism and specific risks can directly and indirectly influence travel intentions through attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control.

### Emerging Travel Trends

Emerging trends in travel preferences highlight a growing appetite for adventure and personal growth. Off-grid destinations are gaining popularity, as travelers seek authentic experiences away from overcrowded hotspots. This trend is particularly evident among younger travelers, who are more likely to structure trips around interest-based events such as sports, wellness, and music. These trends reflect a broader shift in how travelers value experiences over material possessions and a desire to align travel with personal values and passions.

## Conclusion

The balance between risk assessment and exploration desire is a complex and dynamic process that influences travel decisions. Global uncertainties, demographic factors, safety and health protocols, psychological and behavioral factors, and emerging travel trends all play a significant role in shaping these decisions. Understanding these factors is crucial for the travel industry to effectively market destinations and restore traveler confidence in a post-pandemic world.

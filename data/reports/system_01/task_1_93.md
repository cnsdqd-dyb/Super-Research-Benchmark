### Key Integration Mechanisms:
1. **Memory Priming by Reviews**

3. **Attribute Emphasis Shift**  
   - Reviews dynamically reshape what past experiences matter:  
     - A review highlighting sustainable hotels might make a past eco-friendly trip feel more relevant.

4. **Narrative Reconstruction**  
   - Individuals mentally edit memories using **review themes**:  
     - Generalizing a single bad hotel experience to "the destination" if reviews confirm similar issues.  
     - Purposely focusing on positive memories ("I did have great snorkeling") to counter negative reviews.

5. **Risk Mitigation**  
   - Reviews compensate for unreliable personal memories:  
     - Visuals/videos in reviews trust-dump over vague, idealized past recollections.  
     - Reviews fill gaps where memories are faded or incomplete ("I can’t quite recall the café’s quality").

**Mathematical Models:**

The following mathematical models were used to optimize the capacity configuration of different travel modes:

$$\text{Minimize} \quad \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^m x_{ij} \cdot c_{ij}$$
subject to:
$$\sum_{i=1}^n x_{ij} \leq d_j \quad \forall j$$
$$\sum_{j=1}^m x_{ij} \leq s_i \quad \forall i$$
$$x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i,j$$

where $x_{ij}$ is the number of vehicles of type $i$ assigned to route $j$, $c_{ij}$ is the cost of assigning a vehicle of type $i$ to route $j$, $d_j$ is the demand for route $j$, $s_i$ is the supply of vehicles of type $i$, and $n$ and $m$ are the number of vehicle types and routes, respectively.

The following equation was used to calculate the carbon emissions from each travel mode:

$$\text{Emissions} = \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^m x_{ij} \cdot e_{ij}$$
where $e_{ij}$ is the emission coefficient for vehicle type $i$ on route $j$.

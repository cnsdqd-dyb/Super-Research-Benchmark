**How do local perturbations from lane-changing behavior lead to cascading capacity bottlenecks?**

**Abstract**

Lane-changing behavior is a critical aspect of traffic flow that can significantly impact the capacity of road networks. Local perturbations from lane-changing maneuvers can lead to cascading capacity bottlenecks, resulting in reduced traffic flow and increased congestion. This report provides an in-depth analysis of the relationship between lane-changing behavior and capacity bottlenecks, using a macroscopic approach. We develop a fundamental diagram of lane-changing traffic flow and analyze the impact of lane-changing intensity, road geometry, and proportion of lane-changing traffic on capacity reduction.

**Introduction**

Lane-changing behavior is a common phenomenon in traffic flow, where vehicles change lanes to overtake, merge, or diverge. However, lane-changing maneuvers can disrupt the traffic flow, leading to reduced capacity and increased congestion. The objective of this research is to understand the stationary and dynamic bottleneck effects of lane-changing traffic from a macroscopic point of view.

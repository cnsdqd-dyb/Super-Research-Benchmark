**Theoretical Background:**
The Laplacian matrix $L$ of a graph $G$ is defined as $L = D - A$, where $D$ is the degree matrix and $A$ is the adjacency matrix. The eigenvalues of $L$ are denoted by $\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \ldots, \lambda_n$, where $n$ is the number of nodes in the graph. The smallest eigenvalue, $\lambda_1$, is always 0, and its corresponding eigenvector is the vector of all 1's [2]. The second smallest eigenvalue, $\lambda_2$, is known as the algebraic connectivity of the graph and plays a crucial role in determining the graph's connectivity and robustness [3].

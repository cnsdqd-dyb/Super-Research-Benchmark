**Mathematical Formulation**

The optimization problem can be formulated as follows:

$$\begin{aligned}
\max \quad & \sum_{i=1}^{n} s_i \cdot x_i \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{i=1}^{n} d_i \cdot x_i \leq D \\
& \sum_{i=1}^{n} t_i \cdot x_i \leq T \\
& x_i \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall i \in \{1,2,...,n\}
\end{aligned}$$

where $s_i$ is the satisfaction score of attraction $i$, $d_i$ is the distance between attraction $i$ and the previous attraction, $t_i$ is the time spent at attraction $i$, $D$ is the maximum distance, $T$ is the maximum time, and $x_i$ is a binary variable indicating whether attraction $i$ is visited or not.

The optimization algorithm can be formulated as follows:

$$\begin{aligned}
Q(s,a) \leftarrow Q(s,a) + \alpha \cdot (r + \gamma \cdot \max_{a'} Q(s',a') - Q(s,a))
\end{aligned}$$

where $Q(s,a)$ is the Q-value of state $s$ and action $a$, $r$ is the reward, $\alpha$ is the learning rate, $\gamma$ is the discount factor, and $s'$ is the next state.

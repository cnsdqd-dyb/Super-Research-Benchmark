**Mathematical Formulas:**

The Boltzmann equation for phonon scattering rates is given by:

$$\frac{df}{dt} = \frac{1}{\tau}\left(f_{0} - f\right)$$

where $f$ is the phonon distribution function, $f_{0}$ is the equilibrium phonon distribution function, and $\tau$ is the phonon scattering time.

The electrical resistivity of graphene is given by:

$$\rho = \frac{1}{\sigma} = \frac{1}{ne\mu}$$

where $\rho$ is the electrical resistivity, $\sigma$ is the electrical conductivity, $n$ is the carrier density, $e$ is the elementary charge, and $\mu$ is the carrier mobility.

The thermal conductivity of CNTs is given by:

$$\kappa = \frac{1}{3}C_{v}vl$$

**Title:** The Role of Traditional Entertainment Forms in Maintaining Social Cohesion during Urbanization

**Abstract:**
Urbanization poses significant challenges to social cohesion, as it often leads to the erosion of traditional cultural practices and community bonds. This report explores the role of traditional entertainment forms in maintaining social cohesion during urbanization. Through a comprehensive review of existing literature, we examine the impact of traditional entertainment forms, such as live music, festivals, and cultural events, on social cohesion and community identity. Our analysis reveals that these forms of entertainment play a crucial role in fostering social cohesion, promoting cultural diversity, and enhancing community engagement. We also discuss the challenges and limitations of preserving traditional entertainment forms in the face of urbanization and propose strategies for their promotion and preservation.

**Introduction:**
Urbanization is a complex and multifaceted phenomenon that affects not only the physical environment but also the social and cultural fabric of communities. As cities grow and develop, traditional cultural practices and community bonds are often threatened by the influx of new residents, technologies, and lifestyles. In this context, traditional entertainment forms, such as live music, festivals, and cultural events, can play a vital role in maintaining social cohesion and promoting community identity.

Our analysis is grounded in the concept of social cohesion, which refers to the bonds that hold a community together and promote a sense of belonging and identity among its members [1]. We also draw on the idea of cultural heritage, which encompasses the traditions, customs, and practices that are passed down from one generation to the next [2]. Our framework is further informed by the concept of urbanization, which is characterized by the growth of cities and the transformation of rural areas into urban centers.

**Methodology:**
Our research is based on a comprehensive review of existing literature on traditional entertainment forms, social cohesion, and urbanization. We analyzed a range of sources, including academic articles, books, and reports, to identify the key themes and findings related to our research question.

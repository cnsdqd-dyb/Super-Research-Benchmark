**Mathematical Formulations**

The GEV distribution can be formulated as:

$$
G(x) = \exp\left(-\left(1 + \xi\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)\right)^{-\frac{1}{\xi}}\right)
$$

where $x$ is the variable of interest, $\mu$ is the location parameter, $\sigma$ is the scale parameter, and $\xi$ is the shape parameter.

The POT approach can be formulated as:

$$
F(x) = 1 - \left(1 + \xi\left(\frac{x-u}{\sigma}\right)\right)^{-\frac{1}{\xi}}
$$

where $u$ is the threshold, and $\sigma$ and $\xi$ are the scale and shape parameters, respectively.

The GPR model can be formulated as:

$$
y = f(x) + \epsilon
$$

where $y$ is the response variable, $f(x)$ is the mean function, and $\epsilon$ is the error term.

The LSTM network can be formulated as:

$$
h_t = \sigma(W_x x_t + W_h h_{t-1} + b)
$$

where $h_t$ is the hidden state at time $t$, $x_t$ is the input at time $t$, $W_x$ and $W_h$ are the weight matrices, and $b$ is the bias term.

**Introduction**

Heat stroke is a severe heat-related illness that can be fatal if not treated promptly. Heat stroke warning models use various meteorological variables, such as temperature, humidity, and heat index, to predict heat stroke events. These models can be categorized into two main types: statistical models and machine learning models. Statistical models use traditional statistical techniques, such as regression analysis, to predict heat stroke events. Machine learning models, on the other hand, use advanced algorithms, such as random forest and neural networks, to predict heat stroke events.

**Abstract**

The increasing complexity of global supply chains has led to a growing concern about the resilience of inventory allocation in the face of disruptions. Asymmetric transport delay distribution, where delays are not uniformly distributed across the supply chain, can have a significant impact on the response resilience of inventory allocation. This report provides an in-depth analysis of the impact of asymmetric transport delay distribution on the response resilience of inventory allocation, using a comprehensive review of existing literature.

**Introduction**

The concept of response resilience refers to the ability of a supply chain to respond to disruptions and recover quickly [1]. Inventory allocation is a critical component of supply chain management, and its resilience is essential to ensure that products are available to meet customer demand. Asymmetric transport delay distribution can occur due to various factors, such as traffic congestion, weather conditions, or infrastructure failures [2]. This can lead to delays in the transportation of goods, which can have a ripple effect throughout the supply chain.

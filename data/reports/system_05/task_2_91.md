**What Strategy Guides Marketing Node Selection in Word-of-Mouth Propagation Networks?**

**Abstract**

Word-of-mouth (WOM) marketing is a powerful strategy for spreading information and influencing consumer behavior. In this report, we investigate the strategies that guide marketing node selection in word-of-mouth propagation networks. We analyze various factors, including social network analysis, influencer marketing, and centrality measures, to identify the most effective approaches for selecting marketing nodes. Our research reveals that a combination of these factors can help marketers optimize their WOM marketing efforts and maximize their influence spread.

**Introduction**

Word-of-mouth marketing is a vital component of any marketing strategy, as it leverages the power of social networks to spread information and influence consumer behavior [1]. The goal of WOM marketing is to identify and activate influential nodes in a social network, which can then propagate the marketing message to their connections. However, selecting the most effective marketing nodes is a complex task, requiring a deep understanding of social network dynamics and influencer marketing.

**Social Network Analysis**

Social network analysis (SNA) is a crucial tool for understanding the structure and dynamics of social networks [2]. SNA involves analyzing the relationships between nodes (individuals or organizations) in a network, including their centrality, degree, and betweenness. Centrality measures, such as PageRank, Degree, and Betweenness, can help identify influential nodes in a network [3]. For example, a node with high degree centrality is likely to be a hub of activity, while a node with high betweenness centrality is likely to be a bridge between different clusters.

**Influencer Marketing**

Influencer marketing is a key strategy for identifying and partnering with influential nodes in a social network [4]. Influencers are individuals or organizations with a large following and high engagement rates, making them ideal for spreading marketing messages. However, selecting the right influencers requires careful analysis of their audience demographics, engagement patterns, and content quality [5].

**Centrality Measures**

Centrality measures are mathematical formulas used to quantify the importance of nodes in a network [6]. Common centrality measures include:

* Degree Centrality: $C_D(v) = \frac{deg(v)}{n-1}$, where $deg(v)$ is the degree of node $v$ and $n$ is the number of nodes in the network.
* Betweenness Centrality: $C_B(v) = \frac{\sigma_{st}(v)}{\sigma_{st}}$, where $\sigma_{st}(v)$ is the number of shortest paths between nodes $s$ and $t$ that pass through node $v$, and $\sigma_{st}$ is the total number of shortest paths between nodes $s$ and $t$.
* Eigenvector Centrality: $C_E(v) = \frac{1}{\lambda} \sum_{i=1}^n a_{vi} C_E(i)$, where $a_{vi}$ is the adjacency matrix of the network and $\lambda$ is the eigenvalue of the matrix.

**Conclusion**

Selecting the most effective marketing nodes in a word-of-mouth propagation network requires a combination of social network analysis, influencer marketing, and centrality measures. By analyzing the structure and dynamics of social networks, identifying influential nodes, and partnering with the right influencers, marketers can optimize their WOM marketing efforts and maximize their influence spread. Our research highlights the importance of considering multiple factors when selecting marketing nodes, including centrality measures, audience demographics, and engagement patterns.

## Background

The cooling-off period is a statutory right that enables consumers to withdraw from a contract or sale, usually within a few days of signing, without providing any justification. This period is designed to protect consumers from making impulsive purchases or entering into agreements that they may later regret. The duration of the cooling-off period varies depending on the jurisdiction, with some countries providing a longer period than others.

## Determining the Reasonable Time Limit

The reasonable time limit for the cooling-off period is typically determined by the relevant consumer protection laws and regulations. In the European Union, for example, the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU) provides a 14-day cooling-off period for distance and off-premises contracts [1]. This period begins from the date of delivery of the goods or the conclusion of the service contract.

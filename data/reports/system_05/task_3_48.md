**Mathematical Formulation:**
Let $D_i$ be the demand for product $i$, and $I_i$ be the inventory level of product $i$. The goal is to minimize the total inventory cost, which includes holding costs and stockout costs. The inventory cost function can be formulated as:

$$C = \sum_{i=1}^n (h_iI_i + p_i(D_i - I_i)^+)$$

where $h_i$ is the holding cost per unit of product $i$, $p_i$ is the stockout cost per unit of product $i$, and $n$ is the number of products.

The demand for each product can be forecasted using historical data and advanced technologies such as AI and ML. The forecasted demand can be used to adjust the inventory levels and minimize the inventory cost.

$$D_i = f(I_i, \theta)$$

where $\theta$ is the parameter vector of the demand forecasting model.

The inventory level of each product can be optimized by minimizing the inventory cost function subject to the demand forecasting model.

$$\min_{I_i} C = \sum_{i=1}^n (h_iI_i + p_i(D_i - I_i)^+)$$

subject to

$$D_i = f(I_i, \theta)$$

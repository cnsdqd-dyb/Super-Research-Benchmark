**Theoretical Background:**

The local clustering coefficient $C_i$ of a node $i$ in a graph $G$ is defined as:

$$C_i = \frac{2e_i}{k_i(k_i-1)}$$

where $e_i$ is the number of edges between the neighbors of node $i$, and $k_i$ is the degree of node $i$ [4]. The global clustering coefficient, which measures the overall clustering of the network, is calculated as the average of the local clustering coefficients of all nodes [5].

Isomorphism in cultural semantic networks refers to the similarity of identity construction processes between individuals or groups. The isomorphism group structure can be represented as a graph, where nodes represent individuals or groups, and edges represent the similarity of their identity construction processes [6].

## Introduction

Soil mineralization is a critical process that transforms organic nutrients into inorganic forms, making them available to plants. Microbial kinetic parameters, such as enzyme activity, substrate affinity, and maximum reaction rate, influence the rate of soil mineralization. Understanding the relationship between microbial kinetic parameters and soil mineralization rates is essential for developing effective nutrient management strategies.

## Nutrient Management Strategies

**Impact of UV Irradiation and Humidity Cycling on the Aging of Photovoltaic Backsheet Materials and Sealing Integrity of the Module**

**Abstract**

Photovoltaic (PV) modules are exposed to various environmental stressors, including ultraviolet (UV) irradiation and humidity cycling, which can lead to degradation of the backsheet materials and compromise the sealing integrity of the module. This report provides an in-depth analysis of the impact of UV irradiation and humidity cycling on the aging of PV backsheet materials and the subsequent sealing integrity of the module. The results show that UV irradiation and humidity cycling can cause significant degradation of the backsheet materials, leading to cracking, discoloration, and loss of mechanical properties. The sealing integrity of the module is also compromised, leading to increased moisture ingress and corrosion of the metallic components.

**Introduction**

PV modules are designed to operate for 25 years or more, but environmental stressors such as UV irradiation and humidity cycling can significantly reduce their lifespan. The backsheet material, typically made of polyethylene terephthalate (PET) or polyvinyl fluoride (PVF), is particularly susceptible to degradation due to its exposure to UV radiation and moisture. The degradation of the backsheet material can lead to cracking, discoloration, and loss of mechanical properties, compromising the sealing integrity of the module.

**UV Irradiation**

UV irradiation is a major contributor to the degradation of PV backsheet materials. The UV radiation can cause photo-degradation of the polymer chain, leading to the formation of free radicals and the breakdown of the molecular structure [1]. The UV radiation can also cause the backsheet material to become brittle and prone to cracking. The extent of degradation depends on the intensity and duration of the UV exposure, as well as the type of backsheet material used.

**Humidity Cycling**

Humidity cycling is another significant environmental stressor that can cause degradation of PV backsheet materials. The moisture can penetrate the backsheet material, causing hydrolysis and the breakdown of the polymer chain [2]. The humidity cycling can also cause the backsheet material to become swollen and prone to delamination. The extent of degradation depends on the level of humidity and the duration of exposure.

**Combined Effects of UV Irradiation and Humidity Cycling**

The combined effects of UV irradiation and humidity cycling can lead to synergistic degradation of the backsheet material. The UV radiation can cause the backsheet material to become more prone to moisture ingress, while the humidity cycling can cause the material to become more susceptible to UV degradation [3]. The combined effects can lead to significant degradation of the backsheet material, compromising the sealing integrity of the module.

**Sealing Integrity**

The sealing integrity of the module is critical to preventing moisture ingress and corrosion of the metallic components. The degradation of the backsheet material can compromise the sealing integrity, leading to increased moisture ingress and corrosion [4]. The extent of degradation depends on the level of UV irradiation and humidity cycling, as well as the type of backsheet material used.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, UV irradiation and humidity cycling can cause significant degradation of PV backsheet materials, compromising the sealing integrity of the module. The combined effects of UV irradiation and humidity cycling can lead to synergistic degradation of the backsheet material, increasing the risk of moisture ingress and corrosion. The results of this study highlight the importance of considering the environmental stressors in the design and testing of PV modules.

**Recommendations**

Based on the results of this study, the following recommendations are made:

1. **Use of UV-resistant backsheet materials**: The use of UV-resistant backsheet materials can help to reduce the degradation of the backsheet material due to UV irradiation.
2. **Humidity cycling testing**: Humidity cycling testing should be included in the testing protocol for PV modules to ensure that the backsheet material can withstand the environmental stressors.
3. **Combined testing**: Combined testing of UV irradiation and humidity cycling should be conducted to evaluate the synergistic effects of these environmental stressors.
4. **Design for durability**: The design of PV modules should take into account the environmental stressors and the potential for degradation of the backsheet material.

**Future Work**

Future work should focus on the development of new backsheet materials that can withstand the environmental stressors, as well as the development of new testing protocols that can simulate the combined effects of UV irradiation and humidity cycling.

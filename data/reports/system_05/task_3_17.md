**Mathematical Formulations:**

The Lagrangian function is defined as:

$$
\mathcal{L}(x, \lambda) = f(x) - \lambda(g(x) - c)
$$

where $f(x)$ is the objective function, $g(x)$ is the constraint function, $\lambda$ is the Lagrange multiplier, and $c$ is the constraint value.

The stationary points of the Lagrangian function are found by solving the following equations:

$$
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial x} = 0, \quad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \lambda} = 0
$$

The Lagrange multiplier is updated using the following equation:

$$
\lambda_{k+1} = \lambda_k + \alpha \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \lambda}
$$

where $\alpha$ is the step size and $k$ is the iteration number.

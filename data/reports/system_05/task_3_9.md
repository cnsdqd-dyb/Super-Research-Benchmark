## 3. Dose-Response Curves in Animal Models

Dose-response curves are a crucial tool in predicting the clinical efficacy of antipsychotic drugs. These curves are typically generated in animal models, such as rodents, and provide information on the relationship between the dose of the drug and its effect on behavior or neurochemical markers [2]. The dose-response curve can be described by the following equation:

$$E = \frac{E_{max} \cdot D}{EC_{50} + D}$$

where $E$ is the effect, $E_{max}$ is the maximum effect, $D$ is the dose, and $EC_{50}$ is the dose at which the effect is half of the maximum effect.

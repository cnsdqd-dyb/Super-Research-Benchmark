**Theoretical Frameworks:**
Several theoretical frameworks inform the concept of value translation, including:

1. **Global Localization (GL)**: This framework, proposed by Robertson (1995), emphasizes the importance of incorporating both globally uniform values and local cultural expressions in the communication process to achieve dual objectives [1].

3. **Cosmopolitanism**: This concept, rooted in ancient Greek philosophy, emphasizes the idea of a global community or "kosmopolis," where individuals share a common moral law and are bound by the same universal laws, rights, and duties [3].

The “value translation” mechanism essentially **mediates** between purely local styles and a unified global look.  In practice it “maps” local content or design values into the global design language (and vice versa), so that the interface or product still feels consistent with the international brand while respecting local tastes.  In other words, it implements a kind of **“glocalization”**: core brand/aesthetic elements remain the same worldwide, but the mechanism flexibly adapts peripheral details for each market.  As one guide puts it, the goal is to maintain global consistency **“while also delivering local relevance”** – preserving essential design components yet making adjustments for regional markets ([www.thebigger.com](https://www.thebigger.com/balancing-brand-consistency-and-localization-a-guide-for-global-growth/#:~:text=Your%20strategy%20should%20maintain%20global,adjustments%20for%20different%20local%20markets)) ([www.thebigger.com](https://www.thebigger.com/balancing-brand-consistency-and-localization-a-guide-for-global-growth/#:~:text=Achieving%20this%20balance%20is%20what,with%20people%20around%20the%20world)).  

The effect is to “smooth out” the conflict between local and global.  Top‐tier global products don’t force a choice between the two; they build a **global system designed for local expression** ([silphiumdesign.com](https://silphiumdesign.com/local-vs-global-aesthetics-in-web-design/#:~:text=The%20strategic%20debate%20between%20local,systems%20designed%20for%20local%20expression)).  In short, the value‐translation layer continuously **calibrates or normalizes** local variants so that they fit the international style guide.  This ensures that local encoding (language, symbols, layout preferences, etc.) is respected in context, yet the overall look and feel remain aligned with the agreed international aesthetic.  By doing this, it eases the tension – achieving a balanced design that feels both authentically local and recognizably global ([www.thebigger.com](https://www.thebigger.com/balancing-brand-consistency-and-localization-a-guide-for-global-growth/#:~:text=Achieving%20this%20balance%20is%20what,with%20people%20around%20the%20world)) ([silphiumdesign.com](https://silphiumdesign.com/local-vs-global-aesthetics-in-web-design/#:~:text=The%20strategic%20debate%20between%20local,systems%20designed%20for%20local%20expression)).

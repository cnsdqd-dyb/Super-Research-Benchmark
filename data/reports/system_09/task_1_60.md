# Balancing Heritage and Innovation in Cultural Transformation

- **Preserve the cultural core.**  Traditional culture provides a society’s identity, values and “spiritual bloodline,” and these essential elements must be passed down even as society changes.  In practice this means retaining the deep meanings, moral teachings and artistic spirit of the past while divorcing them from outdated trappings.  As one Chinese policy source explains, we should *“take the essence and discard the dross”* of tradition – keeping its finest points while eliminating what no longer fits modern life ([www.ihchina.cn](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/24303.html#:~:text=%E6%88%91%E4%BB%AC%E5%85%9A%E5%9B%A2%E7%BB%93%E5%B8%A6%E9%A2%86%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E4%B8%8D%E6%87%88%E5%A5%8B%E6%96%97%E3%80%81%E5%88%9B%E9%80%A0%E8%BE%89%E7%85%8C%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%80%E7%99%BE%E5%B9%B4%EF%BC%8C%E4%B9%9F%E6%98%AF%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E5%85%B1%E4%BA%A7%E5%85%9A%E4%BA%BA%E8%87%AA%E8%A7%89%E8%82%A9%E8%B4%9F%E5%8E%86%E5%8F%B2%E8%B4%A3%E4%BB%BB%EF%BC%8C%E5%9D%9A%E6%8C%81%E8%BE%A9%E8%AF%81%E5%94%AF%E7%89%A9%E4%B8%BB%E4%B9%89%E5%92%8C%E5%8E%86%E5%8F%B2%E5%94%AF%E7%89%A9%E4%B8%BB%E4%B9%89%EF%BC%8C%E7%A7%89%E6%8C%81%E5%AE%A2%E8%A7%82%E3%80%81%E7%A7%91%E5%AD%A6%E3%80%81%E7%A4%BC%E6%95%AC%E7%9A%84%E6%80%81%E5%BA%A6%EF%BC%8C%E5%AF%B9%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BC%A0%E7%BB%9F%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E5%8F%96%E5%85%B6%E7%B2%BE%20%E5%8D%8E%E3%80%81%E5%8E%BB%E5%85%B6%E7%B3%9F%E7%B2%95%EF%BC%8C%E6%89%AC%E5%BC%83%E7%BB%A7%E6%89%BF%E3%80%81%E8%BD%AC%E5%8C%96%E5%88%9B%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%8C%E6%BF%80%E5%8A%B1%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E5%84%BF%E5%A5%B3%E7%A0%A5%E7%A0%BA%E5%A5%8B%E8%BF%9B%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%80%E7%99%BE%E5%B9%B4%E3%80%82)).  In Xi Jinping’s words, *“every civilization…must be passed down and protected, and at the same time must keep pace with the times and innovate”* ([www.hljswdx.org.cn](https://www.hljswdx.org.cn/info/1096/13215.htm#:~:text=%E2%80%9C%E6%AF%8F%E4%B8%80%E7%A7%8D%E6%96%87%E6%98%8E%E9%83%BD%E5%BB%B6%E7%BB%AD%E7%9D%80%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%AA%E5%9B%BD%E5%AE%B6%E5%92%8C%E6%B0%91%E6%97%8F%E7%9A%84%E7%B2%BE%E7%A5%9E%E8%A1%80%E8%84%89%EF%BC%8C%E6%97%A2%E9%9C%80%E8%A6%81%E8%96%AA%E7%81%AB%E7%9B%B8%E4%BC%A0%E3%80%81%E4%BB%A3%E4%BB%A3%E5%AE%88%E6%8A%A4%EF%BC%8C%E6%9B%B4%E9%9C%80%E8%A6%81%E4%B8%8E%E6%97%B6%E4%BF%B1%E8%BF%9B%E3%80%81%E5%8B%87%E4%BA%8E%E5%88%9B%E6%96%B0%E3%80%82%E2%80%9D)).  In short, the heritage essence acts as an anchor: it gives continuity and depth to transformation by safeguarding the core cultural “genetic code” even as outward forms change.

The balance is achieved by **dialectical fusion** – consciously blending tradition with modernity. As a result, neither side dominates: culture does not become a fossil nor a mere novelty.  By “holding to the true and innovating” (守正创新), the heritage provides stability and identity while innovation provides vitality and relevance ([www.ihchina.cn](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/24303.html#:~:text=%E5%86%B3%E8%AE%AE%E3%80%8B%EF%BC%8C%E6%B7%B1%E5%88%BB%E6%80%BB%E7%BB%93%E4%BA%86%E5%85%9A%E7%9A%84%E5%8D%81%E5%85%AB%E5%A4%A7%E4%BB%A5%E6%9D%A5%E6%88%91%E4%BB%AC%E5%85%9A%E6%8E%A8%E8%BF%9B%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E5%BB%BA%E8%AE%BE%E7%9A%84%E6%88%98%E7%95%A5%E9%83%A8%E7%BD%B2%E5%92%8C%E9%87%8D%E5%A4%A7%E6%88%90%E5%B0%B1%EF%BC%8C%E5%BC%BA%E8%B0%83%E2%80%9C%E6%8E%A8%E5%8A%A8%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BC%98%E7%A7%80%E4%BC%A0%E7%BB%9F%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E5%88%9B%E9%80%A0%E6%80%A7%E8%BD%AC%E5%8C%96%E3%80%81%E5%88%9B%E6%96%B0%E6%80%A7%E5%8F%91%E5%B1%95%E2%80%9D%E3%80%82%E5%9D%9A%E5%AE%9A%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E8%87%AA%E4%BF%A1%EF%BC%8C%E5%BB%BA%E8%AE%BE%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E5%BC%BA%E5%9B%BD%EF%BC%8C%E9%9C%80%E8%A6%81%E6%88%91%E4%BB%AC%20%E7%BB%93%E5%90%88%E6%96%B0%E7%9A%84%E6%97%B6%E4%BB%A3%E6%9D%A1%E4%BB%B6%E4%BC%A0%E6%89%BF%E5%A5%BD%E3%80%81%E5%BC%98%E6%89%AC%E5%A5%BD%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BC%98%E7%A7%80%E4%BC%A0%E7%BB%9F%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%EF%BC%8C%E5%AE%88%E6%AD%A3%E5%88%9B%E6%96%B0%E3%80%81%E6%8E%A8%E9%99%88%E5%87%BA%E6%96%B0%EF%BC%8C%E8%AE%A9%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E6%96%87%E5%8C%96%E7%BB%BD%E6%94%BE%E5%87%BA%E6%96%B0%E7%9A%84%E6%97%B6%E4%BB%A3%E5%85%89%E5%BD%A9%E3%80%82)) ([en.theorychina.org.cn](https://en.theorychina.org.cn/c/2023-11-24/1487547.shtml#:~:text=and%20new%20forms,Cultural)).

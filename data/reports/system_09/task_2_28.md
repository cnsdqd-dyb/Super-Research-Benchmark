# Motion-Artifact Suppression in Wearable Monitors

Wearable heart-rate and vital-sign devices employ a variety of DSP methods to separate true physiological signals from motion-induced noise.  Common approaches include:

- **Adaptive Noise Cancellation (ANC)**:  Iterative filters such as LMS, NLMS or RLS use a reference input (e.g. accelerometer, gyroscope, or another correlated signal) to cancel motion artifacts. For example, a 5 Hz low-pass filter followed by an RLS adaptive filter with a motion-related reference (like impedance pneumography or a 3-axis accelerometer) can greatly reduce baseline wander while preserving the ECG/PPG waveform ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9332869/#:~:text=Least%20mean%20squares%20,Furthermore%2C%20the%20forgetting)) ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=inputs,noised%20PPG)).  Adaptive ANC is simple and fast to implement on-chip, though its performance depends on the quality of the reference and filter tuning ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9332869/#:~:text=Least%20mean%20squares%20,Furthermore%2C%20the%20forgetting)) ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=inputs,noised%20PPG)).

- **Sensor Fusion / Multi-Channel Processing**:  Modern wearables often incorporate multiple optical channels or wavelengths (e.g. green, red, IR PPG sensors) and/or co-registered IMUs.  Multi‐wavelength or multi‐position PPG data can be combined by blind-source methods (ICA/PCA) to separate heart-pulse components from motion noise ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=If%20the%20event%20of%20motion,FSA%29%20are%20studied)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=applying%20ICA%20and%20a%20truncated,we%20implement%20PCA%20with%20SVD)).  For instance, a 12-channel PPG array feeding an ICA/SVD pipeline can identify and extract the most “pulsatile” component as the clean signal ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=applying%20ICA%20and%20a%20truncated,we%20implement%20PCA%20with%20SVD)).  Bottle‐neck techniques like PCA/SVD or ICA require some computation, but when implemented in optimized DSP hardware (or offloaded to a paired smartphone) they can significantly improve robustness to large motions ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=If%20the%20event%20of%20motion,FSA%29%20are%20studied)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=applying%20ICA%20and%20a%20truncated,we%20implement%20PCA%20with%20SVD)).

- **Frequency‐Domain Filtering (Fourier/Notch)**:  Many algorithms exploit the quasi-periodic nature of heartbeats.  A cycle-by-cycle Fourier series analysis rebuilds the PPG/ECG using only the fundamental heartbeat frequency and harmonics, rejecting other spectral components ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=Fourier%20series%20analysis%20,165%20Raj%20et%20al)).  Other methods perform real-time spectral tracking: for each time window, compute the PPG and motion-sensor spectra, identify peaks (heart-rate vs. artifact), and reconstruct the clean signal (e.g. the SpaMA algorithm) ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4732043/#:~:text=PPG%20signals%20and%20HR%20based,12%20subjects%20while%20performing%20treadmill)).  In practice this often manifests as band-pass and tunable notch filters locked to the estimated heart rate and its harmonics ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=inputs,noised%20PPG)) ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4732043/#:~:text=reconstruction%20%28SpaMA%29,12%20subjects%20while%20performing%20treadmill)).  Such filters are computationally light (multiplications/additions per sample) and thus power-efficient.

- **Time–Frequency and Transform Methods**:  Wavelet denoising and related transforms are used to suppress motion noise at specific scales.  For example, discrete (or dual-tree complex) wavelet transforms can isolate the pulse waveform and remove baseline drifts; empirical mode decomposition (EMD) or ensemble EMD can decompose the PPG into intrinsic mode functions and drop artifact-dominated modes ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=traditional%20signal%20processing%20methods,mixing%20problem)).  These techniques can be effective, but often require frame-by-frame computation and thresholding.  Many wearable systems use a cheap 0.4–5 Hz band-pass filter (often implemented in analog or fixed-point DSP) as a first step, then apply higher-complexity processing only if needed ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9332869/#:~:text=pass%20filter%20and%20then%20fed,motion%20artifacts%20and%20achieve%20minimal)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=The%20normal%20frequency%20range%20for,PPG%20signals%20have%20been%20proposed)).  Median or moving-average filters are sometimes used for spike removal or baseline wander with very low cost, although their standalone effectiveness is limited ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=The%20normal%20frequency%20range%20for,PPG%20signals%20have%20been%20proposed)).

- **Machine Learning / AI**:  In research prototypes, neural nets and classifiers have been applied to motion cleanup.  Convolutional autoencoders or time-delay neural nets can be trained on clean vs. corrupted segments to “inpaint” or classify signal portions ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=On%20the%20other%20hand%2C%20learning,using%20stored%20reference%20features%20is)) ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=The%20learning,Finally)).  For example, a neural network might take PPG pulse features and output a reconstructed clean waveform ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=On%20the%20other%20hand%2C%20learning,using%20stored%20reference%20features%20is)), or an SVM might tag sections as artifactual ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=The%20learning,Finally)).  These approaches can achieve high accuracy, but they tend to be too intensive for on-device use unless the model is very lightweight (or inference is done intermittently or on a paired phone). 

- **Low-Power and Sparse Sampling**:  To fit within tight power budgets, wearable algorithms are often simplified or duty-cycled.  For instance, **compressive sensing (CS)** or sparse sampling can reduce ADC and CPU work by acquiring fewer samples of the relatively smooth ECG/PPG waveform ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/15/3097?type=check_update&version=1#:~:text=operates%20effectively%20with%20sparse%20signals,lowering%20the%20computational%20load%2C%20and)).  In CS, the device samples below Nyquist and later reconstructs the signal by exploiting its sparsity, cutting transmission and processing costs.  This must be balanced against the extra computations needed to reconstruct, but it is a promising tactic for ultra-low-power wearable design ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/15/3097?type=check_update&version=1#:~:text=operates%20effectively%20with%20sparse%20signals,lowering%20the%20computational%20load%2C%20and)).  More generally, many wearables perform most filtering in hardware (chopping amplifiers, analog low-pass) and use simple integer DSP routines (IIR filters, moving averages, look-up tables) to minimize the MCU load.

In practice, a combination of the above is used.  For example, a smartwatch might first use a band-pass filter to remove baseline drift, then run an LMS‐based ANC with its accelerometer as reference ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=inputs,noised%20PPG)), and finally apply a small Kalman or FFT-based tracker to refine the heart rate.  Each stage is chosen to balance artifact suppression against computing cost so that the device can continuously monitor vitals within its power budget ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=The%20normal%20frequency%20range%20for,PPG%20signals%20have%20been%20proposed)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/15/3097?type=check_update&version=1#:~:text=operates%20effectively%20with%20sparse%20signals,lowering%20the%20computational%20load%2C%20and)).

**Sources:** Recent wearable-sensor studies report using adaptive filtering (LMS, RLS) with inertial or physiological references ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9332869/#:~:text=Least%20mean%20squares%20,Furthermore%2C%20the%20forgetting)) ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=inputs,noised%20PPG)), blind source separation (ICA/PCA/SVD) on multi-channel PPG ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=If%20the%20event%20of%20motion,FSA%29%20are%20studied)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=applying%20ICA%20and%20a%20truncated,we%20implement%20PCA%20with%20SVD)), Fourier-based reconstruction and notch filters to isolate the pulse frequency ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=Fourier%20series%20analysis%20,165%20Raj%20et%20al)) ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4732043/#:~:text=PPG%20signals%20and%20HR%20based,12%20subjects%20while%20performing%20treadmill)), wavelet/EMD denoising, and machine-learning models (neural nets/SVM) for classification or reconstruction ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6696451/#:~:text=traditional%20signal%20processing%20methods,mixing%20problem)) ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/felec.2021.685513/full#:~:text=On%20the%20other%20hand%2C%20learning,using%20stored%20reference%20features%20is)). Low-power considerations often favor simpler filters (finite-impulse filtering, pulse templates) or sparse sampling strategies ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/5/1493/htm#:~:text=The%20normal%20frequency%20range%20for,PPG%20signals%20have%20been%20proposed)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/13/15/3097?type=check_update&version=1#:~:text=operates%20effectively%20with%20sparse%20signals,lowering%20the%20computational%20load%2C%20and)). All cited reviews and studies discuss these methods and their trade-offs.

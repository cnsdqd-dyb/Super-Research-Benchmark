# Traditional Entertainment and Social Cohesion in Urbanization

As rural communities shift to cities, shared cultural anchors help mitigate social fragmentation.  Traditional entertainment (folk music, dance, theatre, storytelling, festivals, etc.) preserves a group’s heritage and identity amid an urban setting.  Cultural identity acts like a “sense of belonging” – safeguarding **traditional beliefs and native culture** gives people a feeling of rootedness and pride even in a heterogeneous city ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/built-environment/articles/10.3389/fbuil.2025.1582670/full#:~:text=According%20to%20Schwartz%20et%20al,esteem)).  In other words, engaging with heritage art forms reminds migrants (and their children) of their common origins, reinforcing community identity ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/built-environment/articles/10.3389/fbuil.2025.1582670/full#:~:text=According%20to%20Schwartz%20et%20al,esteem)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=inclusive%20,26%2C9)).  Scholars note that maintaining visible elements of one’s culture (rituals, costumes, art) contributes directly to **social cohesion** in cities – heritage “provides communities with places to interact and learn about local culture,” and higher *awareness* and *use* of heritage sites and festivals corresponds to stronger cohesion ([www.sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0264275124001999#:~:text=using%20a%20hierarchical%20linear%20model,better%20living%20environment%20and%20more)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=inclusive%20,26%2C9)).  By connecting people to shared history and values, traditional entertainment builds social capital and mutual understanding ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=inclusive%20,26%2C9)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=This%20eventually%20will%20help%20to,and%20engagement%20and%20aiding%20integration)).

- **Community Bonds through Shared Celebrations.**  Festivals, fairs, and performances built on local tradition create public spaces where diverse city-dwellers interact.  Participating in a familiar street festival or folk concert brings neighbors together regardless of background.  Research shows that such arts/cultural events “significantly boost social cohesion by providing a platform for diverse groups to engage and share cultural experiences, thus strengthening community ties and civic engagement” ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381128992_Role_of_Arts_and_Cultural_Events_in_Community_Development_and_Social_Cohesion#:~:text=match%20at%20L135%20boosted%20social,to%20engage%20and%20share%20cultural)).  For example, a traditional harvest festival in a city square invites both new and long-time residents to celebrate the season, reinforcing collective identity.  These events also stimulate **social revitalization** and pride: they attract local businesses and revitalization efforts, which in turn make neighborhoods safer and more cohesive ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381128992_Role_of_Arts_and_Cultural_Events_in_Community_Development_and_Social_Cohesion#:~:text=match%20at%20L135%20boosted%20social,to%20engage%20and%20share%20cultural)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=This%20eventually%20will%20help%20to,being%20%5B%2072%5D%2C%20a%20key)). 

- **Intergenerational Continuity and Cultural Learning.**  Urban traditional entertainment often bridges generations.  Older community members pass down songs, stories and dances to youth in formal performances or informal family gatherings.  Studies of urban folklore movements (e.g. in Vilnius) show grandparents bringing grandchildren into concert halls and street events where they once performed ([www.journals.vu.lt](https://www.journals.vu.lt/td/article/view/29011#:~:text=already%20become%20grandparents%2C%20bring%20their,music%20labs%2C%20and%20artistic%20workshops)).  This keeps traditions alive and gives younger people a direct connection to their heritage.  Meanwhile, youth also introduce new artistic currents, creating a dynamic “urban folk tradition” that blends old and new.  Such intergenerational transmission preserves a **cultural lifeline** in fast-changing cities; it also creates social settings (families and neighbors attending festivals together) that nurture trust and identity across age groups ([www.journals.vu.lt](https://www.journals.vu.lt/td/article/view/29011#:~:text=already%20become%20grandparents%2C%20bring%20their,music%20labs%2C%20and%20artistic%20workshops)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=This%20eventually%20will%20help%20to,being%20%5B%2072%5D%2C%20a%20key)).

- **Inclusivity, Integration and Well-Being.**  Traditional cultural events can ease the integration of newcomers.  Immigrants or rural migrants participate in or attend familiar dances or music, finding community among co-nationals and also inviting others to learn about their culture.  In turn, sharing these practices with new audiences promotes cross-cultural appreciation.  Because traditional entertainments are often participatory (dance, song, communal games), they provide an **inclusive framework for engagement** ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=minimizing%20disparities%20at%20the%20same,being%20%5B%2072%5D%2C%20a%20key)).  Social scientists observe that cultural legacies offer a “framework for involvement” that aids **social integration and social capital** ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=This%20eventually%20will%20help%20to,being%20%5B%2072%5D%2C%20a%20key)).  

Participation in communal cultural activities also boosts psychological well-being and civic engagement.  Arts and festivals give people a sense of joy, reduce isolation, and create moments of collective effervescence that bind neighbors.  Reviews find that attendees of cultural events report improved mental health and community pride ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381128992_Role_of_Arts_and_Cultural_Events_in_Community_Development_and_Social_Cohesion#:~:text=match%20at%20L135%20boosted%20social,to%20engage%20and%20share%20cultural)).  In sum, keeping traditional entertainment alive in cities – whether via government support of festivals or grassroots folk clubs – helps cities remain **“inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable”**, as cultural planners note the wisdom of integrating heritage into urban development ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=inclusive%20,26%2C9)) ([www.mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/11/9/1508#:~:text=This%20eventually%20will%20help%20to,being%20%5B%2072%5D%2C%20a%20key)).

In summary, traditional entertainment forms act as social glue in the urban context.  They *anchor* cultural identity and belonging, *create spaces* for communal celebration, *connect* generations, and *bridge* diverse groups.  By keeping shared heritage visible and participatory, these arts nurture social cohesion at a time when urban life can otherwise feel fragmented ([www.frontiersin.org](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/built-environment/articles/10.3389/fbuil.2025.1582670/full#:~:text=According%20to%20Schwartz%20et%20al,esteem)) ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381128992_Role_of_Arts_and_Cultural_Events_in_Community_Development_and_Social_Cohesion#:~:text=match%20at%20L135%20boosted%20social,to%20engage%20and%20share%20cultural)).

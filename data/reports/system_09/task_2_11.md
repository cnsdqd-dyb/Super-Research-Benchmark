A promising solution is to **“smooth out” the doping profile near the gate/channel interface by inserting lightly‐doped (or undoped) spacer layers** in the epitaxial stack.  In practice, one adds a thin undoped-GaN (u-GaN) or AlN layer under the p-GaN gate or atop the AlGaN barrier.  This effectively breaks up the steep Mg-doping gradient at the gate and spreads the electric field, which in turn suppresses interface trap effects and leakage.  For example, Zhou et al. showed that adding an extra unintentionally-doped GaN layer beside the p-GaN **“reduced hole concentration near the Schottky interface,”** cutting gate leakage and raising the gate breakdown voltage from ~10.6 V to ~14.6 V **“with negligible influence on the threshold voltage and on-resistance.”** ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352117360_Improved_Gate_Reliability_of_p-GaN_Gate_HEMTs_by_Gate_Doping_Engineering#:~:text=We%20present%20a%20novel%20p,any%20other%20additional%20process%20steps)).  In other words, the device becomes much more robust electrically, but R<sub>ON</sub> stays essentially unchanged.  

Likewise, composite‐barrier designs have demonstrated large gains in both conduction and reliability.  In a recent GaN HEMT study, a **p-GaN/GaN/AlN/AlGaN composite stack** (with a u-GaN spacer and an added AlN layer) achieved ~37% higher drain current and ~23% lower R<sub>ON</sub> than a conventional p-GaN/AlN/AlGaN device ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379790904_Improvement_Performance_of_p-GaN_Gate_High-Electron-Mobility_Transistors_with_GaNAlNAlGaN_Barrier_Structure#:~:text=conventional%20p,Device%20A%2C%20the%20composite%20barrier)).  The undoped GaN spacer “effectively blocked Mg diffusion into the AlGaN layer,” reducing a trap-assisted “P-i-N” leakage path ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379790904_Improvement_Performance_of_p-GaN_Gate_High-Electron-Mobility_Transistors_with_GaNAlNAlGaN_Barrier_Structure#:~:text=conventional%20p,Device%20A%2C%20the%20composite%20barrier)).  In fact, the authors explicitly note that the **thicker, composite barrier (with the u-GaN layer) “is found to suppress traps within the AlGaN layer and reduce … leakage.”** ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379790904_Improvement_Performance_of_p-GaN_Gate_High-Electron-Mobility_Transistors_with_GaNAlNAlGaN_Barrier_Structure#:~:text=and)).  In short, by grading the doping and decoupling the heavy p-type region from the channel, one mitigates the synergistic trap-induced reliability problems while still maintaining a high carrier density in the channel. 

**Key point:**  An epitaxial design that uses undoped/lightly-doped GaN (and/orAlN) interlayers under the p-GaN gate forms a gentler doping gradient and longer‐spacers the high-field regions.  This “graded‐barrier” approach balances low on-resistance with improved reliability – the device keeps a high 2DEG density (low R<sub>ON</sub>) while interface traps and hard breakdown are greatly reduced ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352117360_Improved_Gate_Reliability_of_p-GaN_Gate_HEMTs_by_Gate_Doping_Engineering#:~:text=We%20present%20a%20novel%20p,any%20other%20additional%20process%20steps)) ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379790904_Improvement_Performance_of_p-GaN_Gate_High-Electron-Mobility_Transistors_with_GaNAlNAlGaN_Barrier_Structure#:~:text=and)).

**Answer:**  Use dopant‐profile engineering in the GaN/AlGaN stack – e.g. insert a lightly doped/unintentionally doped GaN spacer (and an AlN layer) under the p‐GaN gate – to smooth the doping gradient.  This structural design reduces interface trap charging (boosting breakdown and stability) without significantly raising R<sub>ON</sub> ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352117360_Improved_Gate_Reliability_of_p-GaN_Gate_HEMTs_by_Gate_Doping_Engineering#:~:text=We%20present%20a%20novel%20p,any%20other%20additional%20process%20steps)) ([www.researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379790904_Improvement_Performance_of_p-GaN_Gate_High-Electron-Mobility_Transistors_with_GaNAlNAlGaN_Barrier_Structure#:~:text=and)).

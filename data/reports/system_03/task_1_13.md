\section*{Executive Summary}
This report evaluates viral titer measurement technologies for optimizing safety and scale in gene therapy manufacturing, focusing on constraints of detection sensitivity and automation. Analysis of current industry practices, regulatory guidelines, and technological innovations reveals that \textbf{Biolayer Interferometry (BLI)} with serotype-agnostic probes (e.g., AAVX) represents the most effective solution. BLI offers high sensitivity ($5 \times 10^6 - 1 \times 10^{13}$ vp/mL), full/empty capsid differentiation, and full automation compatibility, reducing assay time from hours to minutes while meeting stringent FDA validation requirements. Traditional PCR and ELISA methods are limited by sensitivity constraints, matrix interference, and manual workflows that impede scalability. Integration of automated platforms (e.g., Hamilton liquid handlers) with BLI aligns with the FDA's lifecycle approach to process validation, enabling robust control strategies for GMP production. Recommendations include adopting BLI for in-process testing and implementing AI-driven analytics for continuous process verification.

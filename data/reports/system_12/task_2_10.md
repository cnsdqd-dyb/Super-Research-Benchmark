**The optimal surface (areal) density of quantum dots (QDs).**

In quantum dot (QD) lasers, quantum confinement in all three dimensions creates a delta-function-like density of states, promoting temperature-insensitive gain, low threshold current density (\(J_{th}\)), and preferential ground-state (GS) lasing for high spectral purity (narrow linewidth, reduced multimode operation).[[1]](http://www.ioffe.ru/Dep_TM/old/qdl.html)[[2]](https://perso.telecom-paristech.fr/grillot/139.pdf)

However, the high surface-to-volume ratio in QDs exacerbates surface state recombination (non-radiative losses at QD facets/sidewalls or parasitic recombination in barriers/wetting layers), which elevates \(J_{th}\) and limits output power via carrier leakage and internal losses.[[1]](http://www.ioffe.ru/Dep_TM/old/qdl.html)[[3]](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/The-theoretical-dependence-of-the-threshold-current-density-on-mesa-width-for-quantum-dot_fig1_3294435)

Theoretical models (e.g., from the Ioffe Institute) show that \(J_{th}\) is minimized at an **optimal QD areal density** (typically \(10^{11}-10^{12}\) cm\(^{-2}\), higher when accounting for charge neutrality violations in QDs). This balances:
- **Gain maximization**: Higher density increases modal gain for GS lasing, enabling low \(J_{th}\) (e.g., <100 A/cm²), spectral purity (suppressing excited-state/multimode lasing), and high output power (>10 W possible with linear light-current curves).[[1]](http://www.ioffe.ru/Dep_TM/old/qdl.html)[[4]](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/605647)
- **Loss minimization**: Avoids excessive inhomogeneous broadening (from size dispersion), spatial hole burning, and surface/barrier recombination that degrade efficiency and power.[[1]](http://www.ioffe.ru/Dep_TM/old/qdl.html)

Deviations increase \(J_{th}\): low density reduces gain; high density boosts losses (e.g., via QD coalescence or elevated carrier escape). Complementary optimizations like QD size dispersion control, p-doping, or barrier engineering further enhance performance under these dual mechanisms.[[1]](http://www.ioffe.ru/Dep_TM/old/qdl.html)[[5]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/263905463_Theory_of_threshold_characteristics_of_quantum_dot_lasers_Effect_of_quantum_dot_parameter_dispersion)

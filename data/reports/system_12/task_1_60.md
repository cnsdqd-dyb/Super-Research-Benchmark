**In the context of transforming traditional culture—particularly as articulated in Chinese cultural policy—the dialectical relationship between heritage essence (the core values, spiritual essence, or "soul" of tradition) and innovation is understood as a unity of opposites: heritage provides roots and identity, while innovation ensures vitality and relevance in modern contexts.**[[1]](https://chinamediaproject.org/the_ccp_dictionary/two-creates)[[2]](https://en.tsinghua-tj.org/news/6013.html)

This balance is primarily achieved through the framework of **"creative transformation and innovative development"** (创造性转化和创新性发展), a concept popularized by Xi Jinping since 2014, emphasizing that traditions must be neither rigidly copied nor discarded but dynamically renewed.[[1]](https://chinamediaproject.org/the_ccp_dictionary/two-creates)[[3]](http://proceedings-online.com/proceedings_series/SH-EDUCATION/ICoSS2021/ICoSS08601.pdf)

### Key Principles for Balancing the Dialectic:
1. **Uphold the Essence (守正, "Guarding the Orthodox")**: Extract and preserve the timeless cultural "genes"—ethical wisdom, aesthetic values, and humanistic spirit—discarding outdated elements while retaining what aligns with contemporary values like harmony and sustainability.[[4]](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/15/16/2846)

2. **Drive Innovation (创新, "Innovation")**: Adapt heritage through new expressions, such as digital technologies, modern design, or fusion with global trends, to meet current social, economic, and technological needs without diluting the core.[[5]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/388018625_Artificial_intelligence_contributes_to_the_creative_transformation_and_innovative_development_of_traditional_Chinese_culture)[[6]](https://en.front-sci.com/index.php/asc/article/view/4144/4448)

3. **Dialectical Unity in Practice**:
   - **Inheritance Fuels Innovation**: Tradition supplies content and authenticity, preventing superficial novelty.[[7]](http://www.clausiuspress.com/assets/default/article/2025/08/19/article_1755599342.pdf)
   - **Innovation Revitalizes Heritage**: Modern forms (e.g., AI in cultural dissemination, heritage-themed tourism) combat stagnation and promote global appeal.[[8]](https://academic.oup.com/dsh/advance-article/doi/10.1093/llc/fqaf137/8364792?searchresult=1)[[9]](https://www.suaspress.org/ojs/index.php/AJSM/article/download/v3n4a01/v3n4a01/521)
   - **Policy Mechanisms**: Government guidelines integrate this into education, media, and industry, as seen in recent pushes for intangible cultural heritage via digital means.[[10]](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202601/1353640.shtml)

### Examples:
- **Industrial Heritage Hotels**: Merging old factories with modern hospitality while honoring cultural narratives.[[4]](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/15/16/2846)
- **Intangible Heritage**: Traditional crafts like Li textiles digitized for global markets.[[11]](http://english.people.com.cn/n3/2025/0207/c90000-20273556.html)

This approach ensures cultural confidence, sustainable development, and dialectical progress, where essence and innovation mutually reinforce each other.[[12]](https://aj.xhu.edu.cn/xhdxxbzskb/article/doi/10.12189/j.issn.1672-8505.2024.02.002)

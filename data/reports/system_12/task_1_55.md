**The relational process is the key mechanism highlighted in relevant branding and economic geography literature.**

This concept emerges prominently in discussions of brand associations and place branding, particularly in Cecilia Pasquinelli's 2013 paper "The Economic Geography of Brand Associations."[[1]](http://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:665281/FULLTEXT01.pdf) Symbolic capital accumulation refers to the buildup of a brand's prestige, legitimacy, and cultural value—often through historical, spatial, or associative networks (e.g., linking a brand like Vespa to Rome's cultural imagery). Consumer identification dynamics involve how individuals actively relate to, interpret, and incorporate these symbolic elements into their self-identity, creating ongoing relational exchanges.[[1]](http://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:665281/FULLTEXT01.pdf)

Brand value assessment models (e.g., consumer-based brand equity frameworks like Keller's CBBE or Aaker's association-based equity) balance these by embedding **relational processes** as a core dynamic:
- **Accumulation side**: Brands build symbolic capital relationally via networks of associations, endorsements, and cultural embedding, turning economic/cultural resources into recognized prestige (per Bourdieu's framework adapted to brands).[[1]](http://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:665281/FULLTEXT01.pdf)
- **Identification side**: Consumers confer value through dynamic identification, where symbolic cues trigger personal resonance, loyalty, or disidentification—measured via surveys on imagery, attitudes, and relationships.[[2]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0969593117308776)
- **Balancing act**: The relational process acts as an iterative feedback loop—one agent's symbolic resources gain value through others' recognition (e.g., consumer endorsement reinforces brand prestige). This is one of three mechanisms noted for accumulation: relational recognition, ecological interplay of associations, and territorial embedding.[[1]](http://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:665281/FULLTEXT01.pdf)

In practice, models like Interbrand's or destination brand equity assessments integrate this via multi-method scoring (financials + consumer metrics on personality/symbolism → equity), ensuring long-term capital buildup aligns with fluid consumer dynamics.[[3]](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Influences-of-symbolic-capital-on-destination-brand-Kim-Bae/77e9ffdb095dd2e7ba1caca3f0a9aaa170f88456) This prevents overemphasis on static prestige by grounding valuation in lived relational exchanges.

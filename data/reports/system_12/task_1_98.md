**In Integrated Information Theory (IIT), subjective experience—termed "qualia"—is not assigned a single "entropy" measure per se, but its structure and informational content are quantified geometrically using relative entropies (Kullback-Leibler divergences) within qualia space (Q), a framework rooted in information geometry.[[1]](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000462)[[3]](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2713405)**

Qualia space Q is a high-dimensional manifold (2<sup>n</sup> dimensions for an <i>n</i>-element system), where each axis represents a possible system state, and points are probability distributions called "repertoires" (specified by submechanisms in a given state). These distributions start from a uniform "potential repertoire" (maximum entropy, representing no information) and are shaped into "actual repertoires" by the system's mechanisms and activity, reducing entropy to encode causal distinctions.[[1]](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000462)

**The geometry of experience emerges from "q-arrows"**: Informational relationships between repertoires of nested submechanisms (<i>m</i> → <i>m ∪ r</i>). The "length" of a q-arrow is **effective information** (<i>ei</i>), the relative entropy:
```
ei(X₀(m ∪ r, x₁) ∥ X₀(m, x₁)) = H[X₀(m ∪ r, x₁) ∥ X₀(m, x₁)]
```
where <i>H[· ∥ ·]</i> is KL divergence (asymmetric "distance" in information geometry, approximating the Fisher-Rao metric for small perturbations), quantifying how much submechanism <i>r</i> sharpens the distribution in context <i>m</i> by ruling out alternatives.[[1]](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000462)

The full **quale** is the shape (polytope-like solid) in Q formed by all q-arrows from the system's submechanisms, uniquely specifying the **quality** of experience (e.g., "redness" as a q-fold from contextual integration of color vs. non-color connections). **Entanglement** (<i>γ</i>) measures irreducible tangling of q-arrows (like Φ for substructures):
```
γ = H[X₀(m ∪ r, x₁) ∥ ∏_k X₀(r_k, x₁)]
```
over the minimum information partition (MIP), creating indivisible "concepts" and "modes" (sub-shapes for modalities/submodalities).[[1]](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000462)

**Quantity** is given by **integrated information Φ**, the "height" of the quale above its MIP base:
```
Φ(x₁) = H[X₀(T, x₁) ∥ ∏_k X₀(M_k, x₁)]
```
where <i>T</i> is the full mechanism.[[1]](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000462) High Φ enables complex geometry; low Φ collapses the shape.

In **IIT 4.0**, this evolves to Φ-structures (simplicial complexes of distinctions/relations with φ<sup>d</sup>/φ<sup>r</sup>), but retains geometric unfolding via intrinsic information (<i>ii</i>, log-deviation from chance repertoires) and irreducibility, with entropy implicit in probability concentrations vs. uniform (max-entropy) distributions.

Thus, "subjective experience entropy" is quantified **relationally** via KL divergences as geometric distances in Q: the manifold's divergences structure the quale's shape, with no holistic entropy of the experience itself—instead, experiences are low-entropy specifications (distinctions) amid high-entropy possibility spaces, bound by integration/exclusion.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_information_theory) Extensions like geometric Φ<sup>G</sup> refine this using information geometry projections.[[6]](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1603583113)

**Hawking radiation, as originally derived by Stephen Hawking in 1974 using semiclassical quantum field theory on a fixed black hole background, has a thermal Planckian spectrum corresponding to a blackbody temperature \( T_H = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B} \), where \( M \) is the black hole mass.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_hole_information_paradox) This thermal nature implies that the radiation is independent of the black hole's formation history, leading to the black hole information paradox: unitary quantum evolution seems violated as information about infalling matter appears lost in incoherent thermal emission.[[2]](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04806)**

**However, refinements like the Parikh-Wilczek tunneling paradigm introduce a non-thermal spectrum by accounting for dynamical self-gravitation and energy conservation during particle emission. Here, the emission probability is \( P \propto \exp\left(-\frac{E}{\tilde{T}}\right) \) with a position-dependent "greybody" temperature \( \tilde{T} \), deviating from pure thermality and allowing radiation to carry information via correlations.[[3]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.7314)[[4]](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7314) Similarly, beyond-geometric-optics treatments of Bogoliubov coefficients yield small non-thermal corrections that reduce to the thermal limit in the semiclassical approximation.[[2]](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04806)**

**Holographic duality, particularly the AdS/CFT correspondence, imposes stronger constraints by mapping bulk black hole evaporation to unitary evolution in a boundary conformal field theory (CFT). The dual CFT is manifestly unitary, forbidding pure thermal radiation that would lead to information loss.[[5]](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.024038)[[6]](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1733664/FULLTEXT01.pdf) This manifests in the **Page curve** for the entanglement entropy \( S_{\text{rad}} \) of the radiation: it rises linearly (thermal-like) until the Page time (\( t \sim M^3 \), ~half-evaporation), then decreases to near-zero, purifying the state.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_hole_information_paradox)[[7]](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.106.066008)**

**The non-thermal spectrum arises because:**
- **Early radiation** (\( t \ll \) Page time) is approximately thermal, with \( S_{\text{rad}} \approx \frac{\text{Area}}{4G} \) from the island formula in holography (replica wormholes "stitch" interior regions to the exterior).[[8]](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/media/imperial-college/research-centres-and-groups/theoretical-physics/msc/dissertations/2020/Johannes-Eskilt-Dissertation.pdf)
- **Late radiation** requires non-thermal multi-particle correlations (non-Gaussian, entangled states) to scramble and release information, ensuring \( S_{\text{rad}} \) follows the decreasing Page curve rather than diverging thermally.[[9]](https://zenodo.org/records/18137699) Single-particle distributions remain near-thermal, but higher-point functions encode bulk quantum gravity effects like islands.[[7]](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.106.066008)

**These features are **constrained by holography** because:**
- The boundary CFT spectrum must be unitary and pure, projecting onto a non-thermal bulk state via the duality dictionary.
- Computations in low-D holographic models (e.g., JT gravity dual to SYK) explicitly show non-thermal late-time radiation matching the Page curve.[[6]](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1733664/FULLTEXT01.pdf)
- Deviations enforce the no-cloning theorem and monogamy of entanglement, preventing thermal factorization.[[5]](https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevD.107.024038)

**In essence, while semiclassical gravity predicts thermality, holographic duality demands non-thermal correlations—reflected in the Page curve—to reconcile Hawking radiation with quantum unitarity, resolving the paradox without remnants or firewalls.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_hole_information_paradox)**

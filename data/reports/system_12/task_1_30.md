**Clinical (radiologic) TNM staging, particularly using multiphase contrast-enhanced CT or MRI, balances these factors in liver cancer (hepatocellular carcinoma, HCC).[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)[[2]](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/liver-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/staging.html)**

In the AJCC/UICC TNM system (8th edition), staging distinguishes **clinical TNM (cTNM)**—based on imaging—and **pathologic TNM (pTNM)**—from resected/transplanted specimens.[[3]](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorstaging.html)[[4]](https://emedicine.medscape.com/article/2007061-overview)[[2]](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/liver-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/staging.html)

### Quantifying Tumor Burden (T Stage)
- **Strength**: Imaging excels here, using OPTN/UNOS or LI-RADS criteria (e.g., arterial hyperenhancement, washout, capsule). Counts definite HCC (class 5/LR-5) for size/number: e.g., cT1 (solitary ≤2-5 cm), cT2 (2-3 tumors ≤3-5 cm), cT3 (≥1 >5 cm or more tumors).[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)
- High **diagnostic specificity** (>90% for LR-5) avoids overtreatment (e.g., transplanting non-HCC nodules).[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)

### Predicting Lymph Node Metastasis (N Stage)
- **Challenge**: Imaging detects suspicious regional LNs (hilar/hepatoduodenal; cN1) but with moderate sensitivity (~50-60%) and variable specificity; small/perihepatic LNs often missed.[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)
- pN1 requires tissue confirmation (explant/resection), but routine LN dissection is rare in HCC (incidence 5-15%) due to morbidity.[[2]](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/liver-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/staging.html)

### Balancing Specificity and Treatment Timing
- **Clinical staging prioritizes speed**: Preop imaging enables rapid decisions (e.g., resection/transplant eligibility via MELD exceptions for cT2), avoiding delays for biopsy/pathology (which risks progression in cirrhosis).[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436) N stage often ignored for listing (not factored in OPTN T criteria) to prevent overtreatment based on uncertain imaging.[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)
- **Trade-off**: High T specificity (strict criteria) vs. lower N accuracy (15-70% cTNM-pTNM discordance); pTNM more precise but post-treatment.[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)
- **Treatment implications**: Early stages (I-II, N0) favor curative therapy; N1 (IVA) shifts to systemic. BCLC integrates with liver function.[[2]](https://www.cancer.org/cancer/types/liver-cancer/detection-diagnosis-staging/staging.html)

This approach ensures timely intervention while minimizing errors in tumor burden assessment.[[1]](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/AJR.21.26436)

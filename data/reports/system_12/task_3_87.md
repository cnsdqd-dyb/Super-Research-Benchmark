**Connecting multi-site heritage in route planning refers to the strategic integration of multiple cultural or historical sites into cohesive tourist itineraries, often modeled as variants of the Tourist Trip Design Problem (TTDP) or Personalized Group Trip Design Problem (PGTDP).**[[1]](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9408/8/8/301)[[2]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214716022000069)

**This approach optimizes the balance between knowledge (cultural value, heritage score, or satisfaction from diverse sites) and fatigue (travel time, distance, congestion, emissions, or cognitive overload) via multi-objective optimization algorithms.**[[1]](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9408/8/8/301)[[3]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360835225009441)

Key mechanisms include:
- **Efficient site linkage**: Algorithms like hybrid Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), metaheuristics (e.g., IMVO, GAN), or Gradient-Boosted Machines cluster and sequence geographically proximate or thematically related sites (e.g., 92+ POIs in heritage old towns), minimizing transitions to reduce physical/mental fatigue while maximizing coverage.[[1]](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9408/8/8/301)[[3]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360835225009441)
- **Constraint handling**: Time budgets (e.g., 100-160 min), emission caps, distance limits, and congestion models prevent overload, with diminishing returns on knowledge (e.g., discounted heritage scores for crowded sites) ensuring sustainable pacing.[[1]](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9408/8/8/301)[[4]](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17538947.2022.2130456)
- **Personalization and dynamics**: Neural networks (e.g., LSTM with attention) or fuzzy systems incorporate user profiles, weather, and sequential dependencies, boosting satisfaction/utility while curbing fatigue from repetitive or exhaustive visits.[[4]](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17538947.2022.2130456)

**Outcomes show superior Pareto fronts (e.g., hypervolume 0.85), with routes achieving high heritage scores (74+%) under tight fatigue constraints, outperforming baselines like PSO or genetic algorithms.**[[1]](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9408/8/8/301) This enables tourists to gain comprehensive knowledge across sites without burnout, promoting resilient heritage tourism.[[3]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360835225009441)

**In admission stratification systems (often referred to as "准入分层系统" or supplier qualification and tiering systems in procurement and SRM contexts), the performance boundary for supplier promotion is typically determined through a structured, score-based evaluation process during periodic reviews.**[[1]](https://www.zdsztech.com/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-supplier-quality-classification-management-system)[[2]](https://www.going-link.com/mes/2371.html)

These systems classify suppliers into tiers (e.g., A/B/C/D or strategic/preferred/qualified/eliminated) based on multi-dimensional KPIs like quality, delivery timeliness, cost competitiveness, service levels, cooperation duration, supply stability, and risk factors.[[3]](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1994757680326275738)[[4]](https://www.8manage.cn/blog/202508/supplier-tiering.html)[[5]](https://scm-blog.com/2014/08/fenlei.html)

### Key Methods for Determining the Boundary:
1. **Score Thresholds (Fixed or Dynamic)**:
   - Suppliers receive a composite performance score (e.g., 0-100) from weighted KPIs.
   - Promotion boundaries are predefined thresholds, such as:
     - Tier A (top/performance leaders): Score ≥ 90
     - Tier B (preferred): 75-89
     - Tier C (qualified): 60-74
   - Example: A supplier in Tier B is promoted to Tier A if their score exceeds the boundary (e.g., ≥90) after review.[[6]](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1921864962797708247)[[7]](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1673870)

2. **Percentile/Quantile-Based Stratification**:
   - Boundaries use statistical distribution, e.g., top 20% (Pareto/80-20 rule) for promotion to elite tiers.
   - Ensures relative ranking: Suppliers outperforming peers (e.g., above 80th percentile) get promoted.[[8]](https://www.strategicsourceror.com/2016/07/supplier-stratification-first-step-to.html)[[9]](https://www.inddist.com/operations/blog/13764451/supplier-performance-scorecards-supplier-stratification-part-2)

3. **Matrix Models (Performance vs. Other Factors)**:
   - 2x2 or 5-tier matrices combine performance with substitutability/spend/risk.
   - E.g., High performance + low substitutability → Strategic tier promotion.[[3]](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1994757680326275738)

### Process Flow:
- **Initial Admission (准入)**: Basic qualification (documents, audits, initial score ≥ threshold, e.g., 60-70 for entry).[[10]](https://www.8manage.cn/blog/202505/supplier-onboarding-8manage-srm.html)
- **Ongoing Evaluation**: Quarterly/annual scoring via SRM software (e.g., auto-classification).[[11]](https://www.woshipm.com/pd/5897294.html)
- **Promotion Decision**: Cross boundary → Upgrade (more business, better terms); below → Demotion/exit.
- **Tools**: SRM platforms automate this with dashboards, alerts, and AI-driven predictions.[[12]](https://www.sedex.com/zh/blog/%E4%BB%80%E4%B9%88%E6%98%AF%E4%BE%9B%E5%BA%94%E5%95%86%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F)

This dynamic approach optimizes resource allocation, reduces risks, and incentivizes improvement. Exact boundaries vary by industry/company (e.g., manufacturing vs. retail), but are customized in SRM systems for objectivity.[[13]](https://www.huoban.com/yx-jiaocheng/Vm2Ed008NJNDl8g0.html)

**No, the spectral radius of trust matrices does not appear to delimit the stability boundary of interaction forms in digital interaction hypergraphs, based on available literature.[[1]](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/cha/article/33/6/063146/2899697/Digital-contact-tracing-on-hypergraphs)[[2]](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.05154v1)**

"Digital interaction hypergraphs" isn't a widely established term, though hypergraphs model group (higher-order) interactions in digital contexts like contact tracing or social networks.[[1]](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/cha/article/33/6/063146/2899697/Digital-contact-tracing-on-hypergraphs)[[3]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S235271102500281X) Trust matrices typically represent pairwise trust in social or recommendation systems, sometimes extended to hypergraphs via factorization or spectral features for prediction tasks.[[2]](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.05154v1)[[4]](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-7343977/v1.pdf)[[5]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.05154)

Spectral radius—the largest eigenvalue's magnitude—matters in hypergraph theory for extremal properties, degree stability, and dynamics like diffusion or synchronization.[[6]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.24354)[[7]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.13664)[[8]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.08276) In opinion/belief dynamics on graphs or hypergraphs, it influences convergence (e.g., if <1 for contraction), but trust matrices are often row-stochastic (spectral radius=1), with the subdominant eigenvalue key for stability.[[9]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/260637070_Models_for_the_Diffusion_of_Beliefs_in_Social_Networks_An_Overview)[[10]](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~mason/papers/BCM-hypergraph2022.pdf)[[11]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.06825)

No sources link these specifically: no paper describes "stability boundary of interaction forms" tied to trust matrix spectral radius in such hypergraphs. Stability in hypergraph dynamics often involves higher-order Laplacians or basin/linear stability, not trust matrices directly.[[12]](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11446277)[[8]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.08276) Conceptually, if trust drives linear updates (e.g., x_{t+1} = T x_t), spectral radius >1 could signal instability, but hypergraphs use tensors, and "interaction forms" is vague/unreferenced.[[7]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.13664)

This may be a niche, emerging, or hypothetical idea—further research could explore it in trust-aware hypergraph dynamics.[[4]](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-7343977/v1.pdf)

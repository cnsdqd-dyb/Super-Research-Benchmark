The core of the user's query lies in the relationship between the fractal dimension and the "distribution of branch points." *   **Branch Point Distribution:** In this state, branch points are minimized or internalized. The system is "poised" at the edge of chaos. ### 3.3 The Disjoint Winged Strange Attractor ($D \approx 1.69$)

### 3.4 The Disjoint Tracery Attractor ($D \approx 1.38$)
*   **Characteristics:** At this low dimension, the system is barely holding together as a complex entity. It resembles a cyclic attractor but with unpredictable leaps.
*   **Implication:** This represents a rigid but fragile cultural identity. The "branch points" are frequent crises that threaten the system's integrity, as it lacks the internal complexity (degrees of freedom) to absorb shocks [cite: 1].

TECHNICAL RESEARCH REPORT
Title: Balancing Therapeutic Response and Seizure Risk in rTMS Treatment for Depression

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report analyzes the critical balance between achieving therapeutic efficacy and maintaining safety with respect to seizure risk in repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) treatment for depression. The analysis is based on comprehensive review of available clinical evidence and safety data.

KEY FINDINGS

1. Safety Profile & Seizure Risk
- The risk of rTMS-induced seizures under standard clinical protocols is approximately 1 in 30,000 treatments (0.003%) [1]
- Majority of reported seizure events occurred in patients with:
  * Pre-existing seizure risk factors
  * Stimulation parameters exceeding safety guidelines
  * Concurrent use of medications lowering seizure threshold [1]

2. Therapeutic Parameters
- Established effective protocols include:
  * High-frequency stimulation of left DLPFC
  * Low-frequency stimulation of right DLPFC
  * Intermittent theta burst stimulation (iTBS) showing non-inferiority to standard protocols [2]

3. Risk Mitigation Strategies
- Regular monitoring of motor threshold (MT)
- Immediate protocol adjustment if MT changes significantly
- Careful screening for seizure risk factors
- Evaluation of concurrent medications, particularly:
  * Antidepressants
  * Antipsychotics
  * Benzodiazepines
  * Medications affecting seizure threshold [1]

4. Treatment Response Metrics
Meta-analysis data shows:
- Combined rTMS with antidepressants demonstrates significant efficacy (SMD = -0.84, 95% CI: -1.19 ~ -0.48) [2]
- Pooled effect sizes range between 0.65 and 1.39 for various protocols

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Protocol Selection
- Begin with conservative stimulation parameters within safety guidelines
- Individual titration based on motor threshold
- Regular reassessment of parameters during treatment course

2. Risk Assessment
- Comprehensive pre-treatment screening
- Ongoing monitoring of seizure risk factors
- Clear documentation of concurrent medications

3. Safety Monitoring
- Regular MT testing
- Immediate protocol adjustment if risk factors change
- Clear emergency protocols in place

CONCLUSION
Successful rTMS treatment requires careful balance between therapeutic response and seizure risk. Evidence supports safety and efficacy when established protocols are followed with appropriate risk monitoring. Individual patient factors must guide protocol selection and modification.

Impact of UV Irradiation and Humidity Cycling on Photovoltaic Backsheet Aging and Module Sealing Integrity

Executive Summary:
This report analyzes how ultraviolet (UV) radiation exposure and humidity cycling affect the degradation of photovoltaic (PV) module backsheet materials and their ability to maintain module sealing integrity. The findings indicate these environmental stressors can significantly impact backsheet durability and module performance through multiple degradation pathways.

Key Findings:

1. UV Radiation Effects:
- UV irradiation is identified as the primary factor causing backsheet aging and cracking [1]
- At UV doses of 60 kWh/m², backsheet elongation at break can be reduced by up to 97.04% [1]
- UV exposure leads to photo-oxidation of backsheet polymers, particularly affecting the outer layer

2. Humidity Impact:
- High moisture levels accelerate UV-induced degradation through:
  * Enhanced oxygen diffusion to encapsulant materials
  * Increased hydrolysis of polymer chains
  * The combined effect is expressed as:
    $Degradation_{total} > Degradation_{UV} + Degradation_{humidity}$

3. Combined Stress Effects:
- Synergistic degradation occurs when UV and humidity are combined:
  * Higher carbonyl formation rates observed
  * Accelerated photo-oxidation of EVA encapsulant
  * More severe mechanical property losses [2]

4. Material-Specific Responses:
- Different backsheet materials show varying degradation patterns:
  * Polyamide (PA) based backsheets show higher oxygen transmission rates
  * PET-core backsheets demonstrate better oxygen barrier properties
  * Material selection significantly impacts long-term durability

5. Impact on Module Performance:
- Backsheet degradation correlates with module power reduction:
  * 8.36% decrease in backsheet reflectivity leads to 3.69% power loss [2]
  * Compromised sealing integrity accelerates internal component degradation

Recommendations:

1. Material Selection:
- Choose backsheet materials based on deployment environment
- Consider UV-resistant polymer formulations for high-insolation areas

2. Testing Protocols:
- Implement combined-accelerated stress testing (C-AST)
- Include both UV and humidity cycling in qualification testing

3. Monitoring:
- Regular inspection of backsheet yellowing index (YI)
- Track mechanical property changes over time

Conclusions:
UV irradiation and humidity cycling significantly impact PV backsheet durability through complex degradation mechanisms. Understanding these interactions is crucial for material selection and lifetime prediction. Combined environmental stresses produce more severe degradation than individual factors alone.
